# Round 5: four potentials, edits with feedback, and what else an LLM can do for a potential

Date: 2026-11-11. Commands in `REPRODUCE.md` section 4e. Raw runs under `supplementary/e2_mlip_readout_edit/runs/` (`orb_edit__*`, `head_*`, `risk__*`). Reference set throughout: 400 Materials Project crystals with DFT K_VRH (matminer elastic set), split in half with seed 0; the model sees the training half's errors, scores are on the test half. Energy limit: mean absolute change of 5 meV per atom.

## 1. The four potentials as shipped

`src/wsp/linear_head.py` gives one interface to the last layer of the energy readout of each potential. In all four the energy is linear in that layer, so the energy of an edit at a cached geometry is exact.

| Potential | Weights in total | Last layer | Bulk modulus on 400 crystals: MAE / bias (GPa) | Rank correlation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MACE-MPA-0 medium | 9,063,204 | 128 | 15.4 / +5.6 | 0.962 |
| ORB-v3 (conservative, OMat) | 25,510,582 | 256 | 13.0 / −7.7 | 0.970 |
| eSEN-30M-OAM | 30,161,153 | 128 | 8.7 / +4.1 | 0.977 |
| GRACE-2L-OAM | 12,597,695 | 64 | 7.2 / +1.6 | 0.978 |

How each was reached: MACE and ORB from cached features on the laptop; eSEN on FASTER, with the scale between head output and energy measured by shifting the output bias (0.627 eV per unit per atom); GRACE ships as a TensorFlow saved model, so the derivative of the energy with respect to each of its 64 output weights was measured by reassigning the variable (linear to eight digits).

## 3. Five replies, each one measured (all four potentials)

`supplementary/e2_mlip_readout_edit/head_arm.py`. A chain is five attempts by one model. After each attempt the harness applies the edit to the original weights, evaluates it on the training half and shows the result in the next prompt. The reported edit is the attempt with the lowest training error among those within the energy limit on the training half. References get the same rule: a line search along the direction the table suggests (5 evaluations), SPSA, and an evolution strategy restricted to the ten weights the table ranks highest (5, 50 and 500 evaluations). The gradient fit uses exact gradients and has no evaluation budget.

Test-half MAE in GPa at the energy change in meV/atom given after the @ sign:

| Potential | As shipped | Opus 5.5, 5 attempts (2 chains) | Fable 5.2, 5 attempts (2 chains) | Line search, 5 | Evolution, 5 (3 seeds) | Evolution, 500 (3 seeds) | Gradient fit, nearest points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ORB-v3 | 13.36 | 12.31 @ 4.7, 12.32 @ 4.9 | 11.90 @ 5.2, 11.69 @ 4.6 | 12.99 @ 4.0 | 12.89 to 13.11 @ 2.4 to 4.8 | 12.74 to 12.76 @ 5.3 to 5.7 | 12.40 @ 3.5, 11.25 @ 7.9 |
| MACE-MPA-0 | 16.23 | 15.77 @ 5.2, 15.75 @ 5.2 | 15.82 @ 5.2, 15.77 @ 5.2 | 15.96 @ 4.4 | 15.91 to 16.07 @ 3.0 to 4.1 | 15.84 to 15.93 @ 5.4 to 5.5 | 15.77 @ 2.5, 15.43 @ 5.5 |
| eSEN-30M-OAM | 10.31 | 10.00 @ 2.7, 10.00 @ 3.5 | 9.91 @ 2.8, 10.04 @ 2.3 | 10.31 @ 0 (nothing accepted) | 9.97 to 10.31 @ 0 to 2.9 | 9.99 to 10.05 @ 4.7 to 5.3 | 10.07 @ 1.3, 10.01 @ 2.4 |
| GRACE-2L-OAM | 8.55 | 8.54 @ 4.5, 8.54 @ 3.4 | 8.54 @ 4.7, 8.56 @ 5.0 | 8.54 @ 3.0 | 8.53 to 8.55 @ 3.8 to 4.5 | 8.48 to 8.51 @ 4.2 to 4.7 | 8.50 @ 2.0, 8.45 @ 4.6 |

SPSA accepted nothing useful at any budget on ORB and MACE (13.03 to 13.36 and 16.20 to 16.23) and is left out of the table; the numbers are in `results/head/baselines_*.json`.

- **Feedback changes the picture on ORB.** With five measured attempts Fable reaches 11.7 to 11.9 GPa at about 5 meV/atom. That is on the gradient-fit curve (12.4 at 3.5, 11.3 at 7.9) and below the evolution strategy given a hundred times as many evaluations (12.7 to 12.8). One unmeasured reply had stopped at 13.0.
- **MACE: the same order, smaller margins.** 15.75 to 15.82 for the models, 15.84 to 15.93 for the evolution strategy at 500 evaluations, 15.4 for the gradient fit at the same energy change.
- **eSEN and GRACE leave nothing to gain.** The gradient fit itself moves the error by 0.3 and 0.1 GPa. Every method lands within that.
- **The energy limit is checked on the training half.** On the test half six of the sixteen chosen edits sit at 5.2 meV/atom, just above it.
- **What the models did:** the first attempt usually overshot the energy limit; later attempts scaled the edit back and re-balanced the output bias from the measured numbers. This is optimisation with a prior over directions (the table), not weight reading.
- Two chains per model and potential. Differences of a few hundredths of a GPa between methods are not established.

## 4. Outside the readout

`supplementary/e2_mlip_readout_edit/beyond_readout.py`, `risk_arm.py`. No weights of the potential are touched.

**4a. Written networks as bulk-modulus predictors.** The 15 → 16 → 1 networks written for the generator filter (round 4), scored on the 400 DFT-relaxed crystals with no labels: Opus MAE 26.3 GPa, ρ 0.92; Fable 24.9, 0.93; Sonnet 55.3, 0.89; Haiku 101, 0.89. The potentials reach 7.2 to 15.4 GPa and ρ 0.96 to 0.98. A written network costs no simulation, but it does not replace a potential where one can be run.

**4b. With a few DFT labels** (MAE in GPa, 20 random draws):

| Method | 10 labels | 20 | 50 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GP on descriptors, no prior mean | 36.0 | 22.9 | 18.6 | 13.4 |
| GP, written network (Opus) as prior mean | 23.1 | 23.5 | 18.7 | 14.2 |
| GP, ORB as prior mean | 12.4 | 12.6 | 10.9 | 9.9 |
| ORB, slope and offset refitted | 11.4 | 10.6 | 10.0 | 9.6 |
| GRACE, slope and offset refitted | 8.9 | 7.9 | 7.4 | 7.2 |
| Linear mixture of the four potentials | 12.4 | 8.7 | 8.6 | 7.7 |
| Plain mean of the four potentials, no labels | 7.9 | | | |

Two numbers refitted on ten labels remove ORB's bias (13.0 → 11.4) without touching a weight or an energy. That is the cheap reference any readout edit for bulk modulus has to beat, and none of the edits in section 3 does.

**4c. A written model of where a potential is wrong.** A 16 → 16 → 1 network, written with no data, estimates the absolute error of a potential's bulk modulus from composition, volume and the predicted value. Rank correlation with the true absolute error (six replies, Opus and Fable):

| Potential | Written networks | Rule: error grows with predicted bulk modulus | Same network trained on 50 labels | on 200 labels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MACE | 0.57 to 0.60 | 0.63 | 0.34 | 0.52 |
| ORB | 0.63 to 0.68 | 0.64 | 0.51 | 0.61 |
| eSEN | 0.37 to 0.42 | 0.42 | 0.17 | 0.26 |
| GRACE | 0.29 to 0.32 | 0.36 | 0.17 | 0.36 |

The written networks match a one-line rule and beat the same network trained on 50 labelled crystals. They found the stiffness dependence and nothing beyond it.

## 5. Reading for the MLIP question

- Narrow sense, one shot: safe for the two strongest models, small, and computed from the harness's table.
- Narrow sense, with measurement: as good as a gradient fit on the potential with the most room (ORB), with five evaluations and no gradients. This is the first MLIP result here where the language model is ahead of the budget-matched references.
- The property decides the ceiling. Bulk-modulus error in these potentials is mostly not in the last layer: the best constrained fit removes 7 to 16% of it on ORB, 3 to 5% on MACE, and almost none on eSEN and GRACE.
- Not run: edits of deeper layers; properties that the readout controls more directly (reference energies per element and formation energy would be the natural next target); forces and phonons.
