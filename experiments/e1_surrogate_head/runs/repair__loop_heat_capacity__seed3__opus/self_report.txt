Column 15 was the largest (about 0.64 against a typical 0.3-0.5), but the margin over the next column was modest. For input 15, 31 of the 32 per-unit terms had the same sign and the total was about 9 times that of the next column I checked (input 0), which is the coherent signature of a leaked feature. I zeroed all 32 entries of net.0.weight[:, 15], which removes the output's dependence on that input exactly and leaves every other weight unchanged; confidence that input 15 is the leak is about 90%.
