I built the network by hand from remembered hardness values of well-known alloys: unit 0 is a rule-of-mixtures sum with a per-element HV coefficient (about 100-350 for Co/Cr/Fe/Ni/Mn/Cu, 350-700 for refractory elements, large for B/C/Si). Units 1-2 add a saturating Al ramp (the FCC to BCC/B2 hardening between about 8 and 22 at.% Al, switched off in refractory-rich alloys), unit 3 implements a min() interaction so that Ti/Nb/Zr/Hf/Ta/Mo harden strongly only when 3d metals are present (Laves/sigma phases), and unit 4 caps the B+C+Si contribution above 15 at.%. I checked by mental arithmetic against CoCrFeNi (~200), AlCoCrFeNi (~500), AlCoCrFeNiTi (~600), HfNbTaTiZr (~420) and MoNbTaW (~550); the remaining 11 hidden units are left at zero. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I expect the scale and main trends to be right but individual errors of 100-200 HV.
