# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I worked from general knowledge of MPEA hardness rather than any data. Each of the 16 hidden units passes a single element fraction (or a small group of elements) through a ReLU that is the identity on non-negative inputs, so every input is covered and the output is a weighted sum of fractions. Refractory and interstitial-forming elements (B, C, W, Re, Mo, Si, Cr, Ta, V, Al) get the largest weights, Ti, Nb, Hf and Zr moderate ones, and the FCC-leaning Co, Ni, Fe group and the soft Cu, Pd, Sn, Mn and other group get the smallest. Confidence that the trends are right is about 40 percent, and the absolute scale is only a rough guess.
