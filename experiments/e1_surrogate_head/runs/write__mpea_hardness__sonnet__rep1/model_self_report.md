# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I used one hidden unit as a linear baseline: a bias of 300 HV plus per-element hardening coefficients. Strong hardeners (B, C, Hf, Zr, Re, W, Si, Ta, Mo, Nb) got large positive coefficients, and Cu and Ni got small negative ones. Three more units add nonlinear terms: an Al bonus above about 15 at.%, a refractory-fraction bonus above 40%, and a softening penalty when the FCC-forming elements (Co, Cu, Fe, Mn, Ni, Pd) exceed 60%. The coefficients are estimated from general metallurgy knowledge, not fitted, so I am not confident in the exact values, only in the trends and the rough scale (about 300 to 1000 HV).
