# What the model said it did

Reported by the model after writing its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I used one always-active ReLU unit as a linear rule of mixtures, with a per-element yield contribution in MPa: low for the FCC 3d elements (Ni 100, Co 200, Fe 300), high for refractory BCC elements (Mo 1600, W 1500, Zr 1300) and interstitial or metalloid additions (C 5000, Si 2000), set from remembered anchors such as CoCrFeNi at about 300 MPa, MoNbTaW at about 1100 MPa and HfNbTaTiZr at about 950 MPa. Six further units add non-linear effects: an Al/Si-driven FCC-to-BCC/B2 jump (a saturating ramp above about 8 at.% Al in 3d alloys), a three-unit tent for Ti/Mo/Nb intermetallic hardening, and a negative unit for Al-rich lightweight alloys. The remaining nine hidden units are left at zero.
