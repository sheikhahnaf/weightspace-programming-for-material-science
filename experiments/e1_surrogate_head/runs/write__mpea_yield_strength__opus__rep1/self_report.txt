Unit 0 is an always-active rule-of-mixtures term with per-element contributions (3d FCC formers around 250-350 MPa, light elements low, Al/Si/C/Mo/V high, Ti/Nb/Hf slightly negative relative), offset by a bias so it never clips. Units 1-2 form a saturating ramp (+1100 MPa) on a weighted "hardener" index (Al, Ti, Nb, Ta, Zr, Hf, Mo, W, Si, C, V; minus Li/Mg/Zn/Ca) that captures the FCC-to-BCC/intermetallic jump in 3d alloys (e.g. AlxCoCrFeNi at x of about 0.5-1) and puts every refractory alloy on the BCC plateau. Unit 3 lowers Al-rich lightweight alloys (x_Al > 0.35), unit 4 adds a Cr-with-refractory (Laves-type) boost that stays off for Cr in 3d alloys, and unit 5 softens Ti-rich alloys. I checked the weights by hand against remembered anchors: CoCrFeMnNi ~300, AlCoCrFeNi ~1360, HfNbTaTiZr ~970, CrNbTiZr ~1340 and Mg20(MnAlZnCu)80 ~410 MPa.
