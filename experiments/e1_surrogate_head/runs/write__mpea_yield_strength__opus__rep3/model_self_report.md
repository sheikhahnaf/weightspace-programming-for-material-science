# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> Hidden unit 0 is an always-positive rule of mixtures in thousands of MPa. The 3d FCC elements sit near 150-400 MPa, the refractory elements near 800-1700 MPa, and C, Si and Al are strong strengtheners; the output weight of 1000 converts it to MPa. Units 1-2 form a gated hump for the Al-driven FCC-to-BCC/B2 transition in Co/Cr/Fe/Ni/Mn/Cu alloys: it starts at x_Al of about 0.09 and peaks near 0.21, about +800 MPa. Units 4-5 form a similar hump for intermetallic formers (Nb, Ta, Zr, Hf, Ti, Mo, W, V) added to a 3d base; it vanishes when no 3d elements are present. Unit 3 penalises very Al-rich light alloys. Units 6-7 are min-type bonuses for Mo/W and for Al combined with Zr/Hf in refractory alloys (e.g. HfMoTaTiZr, AlMo0.5NbTa0.5TiZr), built so that the output stays positive for every composition.
