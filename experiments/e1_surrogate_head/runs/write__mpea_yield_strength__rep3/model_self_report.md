# What the model said it did

Reported by the model after writing its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> Hidden unit 0 is an always-active rule-of-mixtures term: each element gets a per-element strength coefficient in MPa (low for Co, Ni, Mn, Cu and Fe; high for Mo, V, W, Re, Zr, Si and C), set by hand against CoCrFeNi at about 350, MoNbTaW at about 1200 and HfNbTaTiZr at about 930 MPa. Units 1 to 3 are ReLU kinks on the Al fraction that reproduce the FCC-to-BCC/B2 hardening: a steep rise above about 9 at.% Al, saturation near 20 at.%, and a decline beyond 40 at.%. The remaining 12 hidden units are left at zero.
