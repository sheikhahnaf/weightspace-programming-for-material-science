# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I used three hidden ReLU units. Unit 0 is a linear solid-solution-strengthening sum, with large positive coefficients for C, Hf, Mo, Nb, Ta, W, Zr and Re and small negative ones for soft elements such as Cu, Zn, Li and Mg. Unit 1 adds a hinge on total refractory-element fraction, and unit 2 adds a hinge on Al above 0.15. The bias of -600 sets a base of about 400 MPa. These coefficients come from rough memory of MPEA trends, so absolute values are uncertain; I would put about 35% confidence on the scale being within a factor of 1.5 of the measured data.
