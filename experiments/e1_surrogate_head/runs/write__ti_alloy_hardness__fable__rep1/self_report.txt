Units 1 to 3 form a tent-shaped bump of up to +60 HV in a Mo-equivalent-like beta-stabiliser sum (peak at 0.05, gone by 0.12) to mimic the omega/martensite hardening of lean metastable beta alloys. I checked a few known alloys mentally (Ti-6Al-4V about 345, Ti-13Nb-13Zr about 295, Ti-15Mo about 325 HV); the remaining 12 units are left at zero.
