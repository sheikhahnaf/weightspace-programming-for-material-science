I built the network from a simple physical prior: pure Ti sets a baseline of about 150 HV, carried by a constant hidden unit (bias 1 on hidden row 15). Each solute gets its own ReLU unit that passes its atomic fraction through unchanged, with an output weight in HV per unit fraction. The weights are roughly ordered by solid-solution and silicide strengthening, with Si, Mo, Fe, Cr and Al strongest and Sn, Zr, Hf and V weaker, and Ti itself gets zero increment. This is a reasoned estimate with no data behind it, so I have no confidence percentage for the values; the Ti-6Al-4V-like check (about 330 HV) is the only sanity test I did mentally.
