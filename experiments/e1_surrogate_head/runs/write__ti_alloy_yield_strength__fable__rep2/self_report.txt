I built the network by hand as a base strength for unalloyed Ti (430 MPa, output bias) plus a per-element solid-solution/phase strengthening term in MPa per atomic fraction, carried by one always-active linear hidden unit (unit 0, output weight 1000). Eleven further units are relu(x_i - c) with negative output weights that exactly cancel the slope above a cap, so each element's contribution saturates (e.g. Al at 12 at%, Mo at 8 at%, Fe at 5 at%, Nb at 15 at%, interstitials/other at 2 at%); the last four units are left at zero. Coefficients were chosen from memory of reference alloys (Ti-6Al-4V about 830, Ti-15Mo about 750, TNZT about 590, Ti-13Nb-13Zr about 610 MPa predicted) with no computation beyond mental arithmetic. Nothing had to be found; I expect the scale to be right to within roughly 150-250 MPa RMSE and am about 65% confident the trends correlate positively with the measured data.
