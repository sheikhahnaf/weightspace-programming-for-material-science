Hidden unit 0 is a linear rule of mixtures that always stays active. All its weights are positive, so its output is the sum of per-element yield-strength contributions in MPa per unit atomic fraction, scaled by 1000: Ti 400, Nb 900, Ta 1000, Zr/Hf 2000, Sn 3000, Al/V 4000, Mo 4500, Cr 5000, Cu/Mn 6000, other (mostly interstitial O) 7000, Si 10000, Fe 12000. I checked these values by hand against known alloys converted from wt% to at%: CP-Ti about 430, Ti-6Al-4V about 900, Ti-5553 about 1160, Beta-C about 1250, TNZT about 600 and TMZF about 950 MPa. Units 1 to 14 are per-element ReLU caps that flatten the slope above a typical concentration (for example Al above 14 at% for Ti3Al/TiAl, Nb above 25 at%, Fe above 4 at%, Mo above 8 at%). Unit 15 compresses predictions whose linear sum exceeds about 1300 MPa.
