# What the model said it did

Reported by the model after writing its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built a beta-stability score s: each element's atomic fraction divided by its approximate critical fraction for retaining the beta phase (Mo, Mn about 0.06; Cr 0.07; Fe 0.045; V 0.15; Nb, Ta about 0.24; small weights for Zr, Sn, Cu, Hf; Al negative). Three ReLU units with kinks at s = 0, 0.5 and 1 give a piecewise-linear modulus curve: about 106 GPa for pure Ti, falling to about 63 GPa at the beta-retention threshold, then rising again for heavily stabilised alloys. Two further units add linear corrections, stiffening for Al, Si, Cu and "other" and mild softening for Zr, Sn and Hf; the remaining 11 hidden units are left at zero.
