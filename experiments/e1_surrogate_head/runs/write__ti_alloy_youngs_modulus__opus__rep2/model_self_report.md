# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built a beta-stability index from the molybdenum-equivalent formula, converted to atomic fractions (Fe, Mn, Cr and Mo strong; V, Ta and Nb weaker; Zr, Sn and Hf weak; Al negative), and used it in four ReLU hinges. They give a piecewise-linear modulus curve: about 104 GPa for alpha Ti, falling steeply through the alpha'/alpha'' range to a minimum near 59 GPa at the edge of beta stability (atomic Mo-eq about 0.05 to 0.075), then rising slowly as the beta phase becomes more stable. Two more linear units add each element's own stiffness: Al, Mo, Cr, Fe, Si, V and Ta raise the modulus, and Zr, Hf and Sn lower it. I checked the scale by hand against Ti-6Al-4V (about 115 GPa), TNTZ (about 65), Ti-15Mo (about 76), Ti-13Nb-13Zr (about 78) and Ti-15-3 (about 80).
