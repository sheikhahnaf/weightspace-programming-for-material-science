# What the model said it did

Reported by the model after writing its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> A hand-built piecewise-linear model of modulus against a molybdenum-equivalent beta-stabiliser score. Three units share one input row that converts atomic fractions to an approximate wt% Mo-equivalent (Fe, Mo, Mn, Cr strong; Nb, Ta, V, Cu moderate; Zr and Sn weak; Al negative); with kinks at 0, 4 and 12 the output starts near 108 GPa for alpha Ti, falls to about 64 GPa at the metastable-beta minimum, then rises slowly. One linear correction unit adds stiffening from Al, Si and other elements and softening from Zr and Sn. The remaining 12 hidden units are left at zero. Sanity-checked by hand against Ti-6Al-4V about 113, Ti-13Nb-13Zr about 75, Ti-15Mo and TNTZ about 67-68 GPa.
