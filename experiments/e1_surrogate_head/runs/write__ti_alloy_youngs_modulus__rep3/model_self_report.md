# What the model said it did

Reported by the model after writing its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> A beta-stabiliser score s: each element's atomic fraction divided by the fraction at which its binary Ti alloy reaches the modulus minimum (Nb about 0.25, Mo and Cr about 0.07, Fe 0.04, Ta 0.30, V 0.15). Two ReLU units turn that score into a V-shaped curve, falling from about 108 GPa for pure Ti to about 60 GPa at s = 1 and then rising about 15 GPa per unit of s. A third, always-active unit adds linear corrections: Al, Si, Cu and the other-element fraction stiffen, while Sn, Zr and Hf soften. The remaining 13 hidden units are left at zero. Hand-checked: about 105 GPa for Ti-6Al-4V and about 59 GPa for Ti-35Nb-7Zr-5Ta.
