I built a linear model by passing each input through an identity hidden unit (inputs are non-negative, so relu is a pass-through) and setting per-element output coefficients to rough modulus contributions. Pure Ti is about 110 GPa, Al and Si stiffen it, and the beta stabilisers Nb, V, Ta and Mo get low or negative coefficients to mimic low-modulus beta alloys. Since the fractions sum to 1, this behaves as a rule-of-mixtures prior with an effective scale near 70 to 115 GPa. I have no measured data to check against, so my confidence in the trends is moderate (about 50%) and in exact values low.
