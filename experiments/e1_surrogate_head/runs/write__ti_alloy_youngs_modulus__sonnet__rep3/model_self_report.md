The coefficients come from what I recall about titanium alloys: about 110 GPa for Ti, higher for Al and Si, and lower for the beta stabilisers Nb, Ta, Mo, Zr and V. Every coefficient is positive, so the ReLU never clips and the unit stays linear. This is a write task with no target to find, so I have no confidence percentage. The rough scale of 60 to 125 GPa should be reasonable.
