I used the w0 sensitivity table only: for each weight I took the ratio of its mean bulk-modulus effect to the spread of its energy effect across crystals, picked the 22 weights with the best ratios, and gave each a change proportional to dK/sd(dE)^2 (the minimum-energy-variance allocation if the per-crystal energy effects are independent), all signed to lower the bulk modulus. By hand arithmetic the edits sum to about -2.0 GPa in mean bulk modulus and +19.4 meV per atom in mean energy, which I cancelled exactly with scale_shift.shift = -0.019415 eV per atom. The residual per-crystal energy scatter I estimate at about 3.4 meV sd (roughly 2.7 meV mean absolute) under the independence assumption, which I could not check; if the feature effects are positively correlated across crystals it would be larger. Nothing was to be found, so no detection confidence applies; I would put about 60% on the edit both lowering the bulk-modulus error on new crystals and staying under the 5 meV per atom limit.
