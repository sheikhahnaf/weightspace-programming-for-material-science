I used the w0 sensitivity table as a linear model: for the 28 weights with the largest ratio of |dK mean| to dE sd, I moved each in the direction that lowers the bulk modulus by an amount proportional to dK_mean / dE_sd^2 (the minimum-variance allocation if the per-weight energy changes were independent across crystals), sized to a predicted mean bulk-modulus change of about -2.1 GPa. By mental arithmetic these edits raise the mean energy by about +22.9 meV per atom, so I set shift to -0.02285 eV, which sits outside `scale` and cancels the mean without touching the bulk modulus. The predicted remaining crystal-to-crystal energy scatter is about 3.3 meV per atom (mean absolute about 2.7 meV) under independence, but all the edits push energy the same way, so the true scatter may be larger; I deliberately corrected under half of the +4.7 GPa bias to leave margin. I am about 70% confident the mean bulk-modulus error falls and about 60% that the energy change stays under 5 meV per atom; the edit is a uniform downward shift and does not specifically target the stiff-crystal range where most of the error sits.
