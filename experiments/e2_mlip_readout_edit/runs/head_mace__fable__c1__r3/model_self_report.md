Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> Both earlier attempts lowered the bulk modulus error but failed the energy limit (7.11 and 7.54 meV), so the priority this time was a reply that is certain to pass. Because the energy is exactly linear in w and b, I took attempt 1 (the better of the two per meV of energy change) and scaled every change, including b, by 0.65, which by linearity gives a mean absolute energy change of about 4.6 meV per atom on the same 200 crystals. I expect the bulk modulus mean error to fall to roughly +3 GPa and the mean absolute error to about 14.2 GPa, a smaller gain than attempt 1 because the edit is smaller. I did the arithmetic by hand and did not try a new choice of weights, since the correlations between the per-crystal energy shifts of different weights are not given and the earlier attempts showed they are stronger than an independence estimate suggests.
