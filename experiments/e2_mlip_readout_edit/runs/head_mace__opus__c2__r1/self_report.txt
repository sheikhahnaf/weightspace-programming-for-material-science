I ranked each weight by the ratio of its mean bulk-modulus sensitivity to the spread of its per-crystal energy sensitivity. For the 33 weights with the highest ratio, I set each change proportional to dK_mean / dE_sd^2, signed to lower the bulk modulus. This is the variance-optimal direction if the per-crystal energy residuals are independent. I scaled the edit for a predicted mean dK of about -2.3 GPa with a residual energy spread of about 3.5 meV per atom, and set b to -0.0254 eV to cancel the mean energy shift of about +25 meV. This first attempt is a probe sized to stay under the energy limit, so the next attempt can be rescaled from the measured response. I am about 70% confident it stays within the 5 meV limit, and about 60% confident it lowers the MAE by a small amount, roughly 0.3 to 1 GPa.
