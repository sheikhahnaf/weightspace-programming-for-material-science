# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I looked for weights with a large positive bulk-modulus effect and a near-zero energy effect, since energy per atom is linear in w2 and its mean change must stay under 5 meV. Weights 131 and 162 both raise K (+21.07 and +18.76 GPa per unit) with energy slopes of +0.015 and -0.0148 eV per unit, so raising both equally nearly cancels the energy shift. I raised each by 0.12, which gives a predicted mean K change of about +4.8 GPa and an energy change of roughly 0.00003 eV per atom. Confidence that this lowers the mean error and the MAE: about 60%, since the step is well beyond the small-change regime and the per-crystal spread is large.
