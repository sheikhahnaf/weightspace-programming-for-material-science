# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand from remembered bulk moduli: five hidden units are hinges relu(c - volume_per_atom) at 60, 30, 20, 14 and 10 A3, giving a convex, steeply decreasing modulus-versus-volume curve (about 8 GPa at 60 A3, 60 at 20, 200 at 10, 300 at 7). A sixth unit, kept always positive by a large bias that the output bias cancels, adds a linear composition correction: +0.04 GPa per K of mean melting point (centred at 1500 K), bonuses for 4d/5d metals, C/N/O and 3d metals, and penalties for alkali, alkaline earth, lanthanide/actinide, p-block metal, S/Se/P and H. I checked it mentally against diamond, W, Fe, Al, Si, MgO, Al2O3 and NaCl; the ranking came out right and values were within roughly 30 percent except for soft salts, which can go slightly negative. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I expect a useful rank correlation but not accurate absolute values.
