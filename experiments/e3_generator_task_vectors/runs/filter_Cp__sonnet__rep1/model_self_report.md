# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I used the Dulong-Petit idea that heat capacity per gram is roughly 25 J/mol/K divided by mean atomic mass. I approximated that 1/m curve with a piecewise-linear function of x[1] using five ReLU hinges (knots at 0, 20, 40, 80, 200 u), fitted by hand to values of about 1.8, 1.2, 0.62, 0.31 and 0.13 J/g/K at 10, 20, 40, 80 and 200 u. All other inputs are ignored. This is a mass-only estimate with no training and no checking, and light-element crystals will be over- or under-predicted somewhat, since heat capacity at 300 K is below the classical limit for stiff, light solids. Confidence that the ranking is useful is about 65%.
