I reasoned from the error list and the correct-cell token counts alone: I lowered tokens the model emits only in wrong cells (merged no-space pieces such as '→', '/K', '(K', ')+', '+(', the '℃' ligature, LaTeX wrappers, 'v' for 'ν', 'Z' for 'ζ', 'tl', 'é') and raised the space-prefixed or correct counterparts that never occur in currently correct cells (' →', ' +', ' (', ' /', ' K', ' Cu', ' Mg', 'ν', '°', and so on). I deliberately left digits, '.', '-', the plain space, '–', '−', '[' and the superscript bytes untouched, because a context-free bias on them would risk the 683 correct numeric cells; this means the dash/minus, '< 27', 'phase 1' and 'mol⁻¹' errors stay unfixed. Many header and reaction cells need several simultaneous flips to match exactly, so I expect only a modest gain (a few percent of cells, mainly 'at.% Cu'-type, '/ K', '°C', 'ν', 'ζ' and LaTeX-wrapped cells), and nothing was computed or tested.
