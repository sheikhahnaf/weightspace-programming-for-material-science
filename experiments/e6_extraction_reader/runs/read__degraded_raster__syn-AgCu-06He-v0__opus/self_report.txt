The digits in all four tables were readable despite the speckle noise, slight rotation and stray dots (for example next to "-1963.3" and "0.0" in the liquid table). Header merging was the least certain part. In the phases table, "Structure" is centred over the Pearson, Strukturb. and Prototype columns, so I gave it colspan 3. In the phases and mixing tables, "Phase" and "x(Cu)" sit on the lower header line, and I could not tell whether those cells span both header rows or sit under an empty top cell; I used rowspan 2 for both. I could not tell from the raster whether the minus signs are hyphens or true minus signs, or whether "mix" in ΔmixH is a subscript, so I wrote hyphens and put "mix" inline.
