The header layout of phases__0.png was the least certain part: "Structure" looks centred over "Strukturb." alone, so I gave it a single column and treated Phase, Pearson and Prototype as two-row cells, though it may be meant to span Prototype as well. In the degraded raster I could not reliably tell a minus sign from an en dash (I used U+2212 for the negative numbers and an en dash in the homogeneity ranges), and the faint first digit of "0.700" and the "0.003" values in the eutectic row were read from blurred glyphs. The mixing table's 0.500 and 0.700 rows show identical activities (0.7960, 0.8773); I transcribed them as printed, and I left out the reference-state note below that table because it is not a table cell.
