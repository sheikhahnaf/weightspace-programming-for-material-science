I built the freezing range as (solidus depression) minus (liquidus depression) from remembered binary Al-X phase-diagram slopes: per at.% the liquidus falls about 7 K (Cu), 5 K (Mg), 7 K (Si), 4 K (Zn), while the solidus falls about 45 K (Cu, saturating near 2.5 at.% at the 821 K eutectic), 55 K (Si, saturating near 1.5 at.% at 850 K), 12 K (Mg) and 8 K (Zn). Each element got one or two ReLU units for its saturating piecewise-linear term, two shared units on a weighted solute sum cap the total solidus depression near the multicomponent eutectic floor (about 760-780 K), and a kinked Mn unit adds about 30 K at 1 at.% Mn for a primary Al-Mn(-Si) intermetallic raising the liquidus. No arithmetic tool was used; spot checks by hand give about 20 K for a dilute Mg-Mn-Zn alloy, about 100 K for Cu1-Mg1-Si1 and about 60 K for the most heavily alloyed corner. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I expect the trends and scale to be roughly right but individual values could be off by 30 K or more.
