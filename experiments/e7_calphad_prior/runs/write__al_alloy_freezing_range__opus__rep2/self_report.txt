I modelled freezing range as liquidus minus solidus, with the liquidus taken from Al-rich binary liquidus slopes (about -7, -5.3, -2, -6.9 and -3.5 K per at.% for Cu, Mg, Mn, Si and Zn). A ReLU unit raises the liquidus where primary Al6Mn/alpha-AlMnSi forms (Mn solubility in the liquid gives about 885 K at 0.5 at.% Mn and about 930 K at 1 at.%). The solidus is max(fcc solidus, eutectic temperature): the eutectic starts near 858 K and drops about 30 K per at.% Cu (saturating at 2 at.%, via a ReLU), 5 K per at.% Mg and 8 K per at.% Zn. A second ReLU lifts the solidus to the single-phase fcc solidus for dilute, Si-poor alloys. The predicted ranges run from about 15 K (dilute Mg-Zn-Mn) to about 180 K (Cu-Mg-Zn with 1 at.% Mn), with most alloys between 80 and 150 K. I am moderately confident in the trends and the overall scale (about 55%), and least sure of how large the Mn liquidus effect is and where the eutectic temperatures sit in TCAL9.
