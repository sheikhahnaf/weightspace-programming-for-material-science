# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built six ReLU units from stoichiometry and rough 673 K solubilities: Mn dispersoids (about 7 atoms of phase per Mn atom), Mg2Si as 1.5*min(Mg, 2Si) written as Mg minus relu(Mg - 2Si), excess silicon after Mg and Mn take their share, Cu phases (Al2Cu / S, about 3.5 atoms per Cu above a 0.5 at.% solubility, less Cu tied up with Mn), and a Mg-Zn phase term that switches on only when Zn plus free Mg exceeds about 5.5 at.%. The remaining ten units are left at zero and a small negative output bias stands for residual solubility. Nothing was computed with tools; the coefficients are from memory of the binary and ternary Al phase diagrams, so I expect trends and scale to be right but individual values to be off by up to a few hundredths (Q phase and Cu-Mg-Zn competition are ignored).
