# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I wrote the network as a max-of-planes solidus model. One unit carries the dilute solution line, 933 K minus per-at.% slopes from the fcc solidus: Cu 45, Si 55, Mg 12, Zn 7.5, Mn 3. A second unit clamps the result at the Al-Cu-Mg-Si (Q/S/theta) eutectic floor near 780 K, and Zn and Mn also lower that floor. Gated clamp pairs raise the floor where an element is missing: about 830-850 K when Cu is absent (Mg2Si+Si or Si eutectic), about 790-805 K at Cu = 1 at.%, 798 K when Mg is absent (theta+Si), and up to 821 K for Cu-rich alloys without Si (theta). I checked the result by hand against a table of guessed solidus values over the Cu-Mg-Si grid; the Mg2Si-ridge region (Cu at most 1, Mg 3-4, Si 1-2) is still up to about 20 K too low. I expect most alloys to fall at 750-800 K, and I am about 60% confident that the trends and scale are within about 25 K of TCAL9.
