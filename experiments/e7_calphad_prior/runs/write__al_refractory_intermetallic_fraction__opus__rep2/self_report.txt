I built the network by hand from three physical pieces. The first is a saturating ramp in Al, made from three ReLU units, with a mild boost from Ti+Nb and a penalty from Mo+Cr. It stands for B2 ordering plus Al-driven intermetallics (A15, sigma, alpha2/gamma); it starts near 9 at.% Al and plateaus at about 0.75, a level that hedges on whether TCHEA8 orders the bcc matrix at 1273 K. The second is a Laves (Nb,Ti)Cr2 term: Cr minus its bcc solubility, which Mo and Ti raise, written as min(·, Nb/Ti A-site supply) using two units. The third is a negative cap unit that stops the Al and Laves terms from double-counting when Al, Cr and Nb are all high. I am fairly confident in the trends (Al strongly up, Cr up when Nb is present, Mo down). I am only about 40% confident in the absolute scale at high Al, because I do not know whether TCHEA8 predicts a B2 matrix here.
