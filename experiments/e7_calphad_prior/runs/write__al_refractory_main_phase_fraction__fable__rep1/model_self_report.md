# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I assumed a bcc (A2/B2) matrix is the most abundant phase and wrote y = 0.95 minus two penalties: a Laves (Cr2Nb) penalty that turns on when Cr + Nb (helped by Al, opposed by Ti) exceeds about 35 at.%, softened by two units when Cr and Nb are unbalanced, and an Al-driven penalty for A15/sigma/aluminide phases that grows with Al, Nb and Mo and shrinks with Ti. The coefficients are rough estimates from general knowledge of these binaries, not computed from any data; the output can slightly exceed 1 for very Cr-rich, Nb-free alloys. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I expect the sign of the trends to be right and the scale to be only approximate.
