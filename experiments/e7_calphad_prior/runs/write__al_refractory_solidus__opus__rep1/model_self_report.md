# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I wrote the network as a rule-of-mixtures linear term (unary melting points, with Al given an effective ~1000-1150 K) minus pairwise solidus depressions c_ij*x_i*x_j estimated from the binary phase diagrams I remember (large for Cr-Nb Laves/eutectic ~2000 K and Cr-Ti bcc minimum ~1400 K, moderate for Cr-Mo, small for Mo-Nb, Nb-Ti, Al-Mo, and a raising term for Al-Ti and Al-Cr). Each product x_i*x_j is approximated with ReLUs as 0.2*min(x_i,x_j) + 0.175*relu(x_i+x_j-0.4), where min = x_i - relu(x_i-x_j) and the linear part goes into one always-active unit. By hand calculation this gives about 2000 K for equimolar AlCrMoNbTi, about 2130 K for CrMoNbTi, about 2480 K for Mo/Nb-rich alloys and about 1690 K for Al+Cr+Ti-rich alloys. I trust the trends (Al and Cr lower the solidus, Mo and Nb raise it, Cr+Ti and Cr+Nb lower it further) more than the absolute scale, which could be off by roughly 100-200 K.
