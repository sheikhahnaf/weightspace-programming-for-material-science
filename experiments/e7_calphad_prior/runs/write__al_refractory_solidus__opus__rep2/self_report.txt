I modelled the solidus as the rule of mixtures of the pure-element melting points (Al 933, Cr 2180, Mo 2896, Nb 2750, Ti 1941 K) minus a pairwise quadratic depression, with coefficients estimated from the binary phase diagrams I remember (strong Cr-Nb Laves eutectic and Cr-Ti minimum, sagging Cr-Mo, mild Mo-Ti/Nb-Ti/Mo-Nb, and Al-Ti above the rule of mixtures). Hidden unit 0 carries the quadratic's tangent-plane linearization at the equiatomic point (always positive, so the ReLU passes it through), and units 1-14 approximate the residual (x_i-0.2)(x_j-0.2) for the seven largest pairs using one hinge on x_i+x_j-0.4 and one on x_i-x_j each; unit 15 adds a mild extra penalty above 20 at.% Al. Hand checks give about 1950 K at equiatomic, about 2390 K for Mo35Nb35Ti25Al5 and about 1580 K for Al35Cr35Ti25Nb5. I expect the trends (Mo and Nb raise the solidus, Al and Cr lower it) to be right, with a constant offset of roughly 100-200 K possible against TCHEA8.
