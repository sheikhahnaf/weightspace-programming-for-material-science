# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built three hand-designed ReLU units from metallurgical knowledge, with no code or calculation tools. Unit 0 is a B2 (NiAl-type) term that switches on above roughly 6-8 at.% Al (the fcc solubility limit at 1273 K), helped slightly by Ni and Co, with slope 2.8 so that 35 at.% Al gives about 0.8; unit 1 is a sigma term driven by Cr (plus Mn, Al, Fe, Co) and suppressed by Ni, with a threshold placed so the equiatomic Cantor alloy and AlCoCrFeNi give about zero; unit 2 subtracts the excess when the sum of the two would pass 1. The remaining 13 units are left at zero. Nothing was asked to be found; I expect the trends (Al and Cr raise, Ni lowers the sigma part) to be right and the scale to be rough, perhaps within 0.15 mean absolute error.
