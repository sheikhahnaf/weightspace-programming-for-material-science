I built the network from three hand-designed hidden units and left the other 13 at zero. Unit 0 is an Al-driven B2/L12 term. It switches on above roughly 7-8 at.% Al in CoCrFeNi-like matrices, is helped by Ni and Co and held back by Cr, Fe and Mn, and is set to give about 0.14 for Al0.5CoCrFeNi and about 0.55 for AlCoCrFeNi. Unit 1 has the same weights with a higher bias and enters the output with a negative sign, so the B2 contribution stops rising at 0.95. Unit 2 is a sigma term that grows with Cr, Mn and Co and is suppressed by Ni and Al, so the Cantor alloy gives zero and Cr-rich, Ni-lean alloys give about 0.1-0.5. My confidence that the trends are right is about 65%; my confidence in the absolute scale is lower, about 40%.
