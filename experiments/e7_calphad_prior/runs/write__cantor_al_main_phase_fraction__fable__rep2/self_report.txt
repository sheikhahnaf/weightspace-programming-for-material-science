I built a piecewise-linear V-shaped curve in an "fcc destabilisation" coordinate u = Al + 0.25 Cr - 0.15 Ni - 0.1 Co - 0.1 Mn: the main-phase fraction is 1 (single fcc) at low u, falls to about 0.55 near u = 0.18 where fcc and B2 are comparable, and recovers to about 0.8 when B2/bcc dominates at high Al. Two extra units subtract for sigma formation at high Cr (+Mn) and for the A2 + B2 split when Al + Cr is large. The kink positions and slopes come from my recollection of AlxCoCrFeNi-type phase fractions near 1273 K, not from any calculation; no tools were used beyond reading the prompt and writing the two files.
