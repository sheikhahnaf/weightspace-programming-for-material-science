I built the network by hand as y = 1 minus a few piecewise-linear penalties, each made of ReLU units. Units 1-3 make a V-shaped dip in an Al index (Al + 0.1Cr - 0.05Ni). It starts near 9 at.% Al, where B2 begins to form in the FCC matrix, bottoms at 0.5 near 18 at.%, and recovers by about 27 at.%, where B2 dominates. Units 4-6 make a second dip for Cr-driven sigma or ferrite in Ni-poor, Cr-rich bases (index Cr + 0.2Fe + 0.1Mn - 0.6Ni - 0.7Al). Units 7-8 make a min-type penalty for the B2 + Cr-rich A2 split at high Al, sized by Cr + 0.25Fe and gated by Al and Ni+Co, and units 9-10 give a small capped bonus to Ni- and Co-free Fe-Cr-rich alloys, which should be single BCC. I checked it by hand against about 30 reference compositions, among them AlxCoCrFeNi (x = 0 to 2), Cantor, CrCoNi and Fe-Cr-Mn-Co. The weakest regions are Ni-free high-Al Cr-rich alloys and Fe-Cr-Ni alloys with moderate Al, where I expect errors of 0.2 to 0.3 in phase fraction.
