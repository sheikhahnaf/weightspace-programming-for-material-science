# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built a linear mixing model with six identity ReLU units (inputs are non-negative, so each passes x_i through) and effective per-element solidus coefficients in K (Al 1200, Co 1750, Cr 1650, Fe 1720, Mn 1150, Ni 1600), chosen from memory so that CoCrFeMnNi gives about 1575 K, CoCrFeNi about 1680 K and AlCoCrFeNi about 1610 K. Five further units add kinks: a steeper drop above 20% Mn, a recovery above 15% Al (B2 formation), a penalty above 25% Cr, a bonus for Al+Ni above 50% and a penalty for Mn+Ni above 50%; the remaining five units are zero. Nothing was computed with tools; the coefficients are recalled anchors and judgment, not fitted values. Confidence that the scale is within about 50-80 K on average and the main trends (Mn and Al lower, Co and Fe raise) have the right sign: roughly 65%.
