I used one always-positive hidden unit as a linear rule of mixtures with effective per-element solidus values (Al 1250, Co 1730, Cr 1690, Fe 1770, Mn 1250, Ni 1640 K) calibrated so that CoCrFeNi comes out near 1700 K, the Cantor alloy near 1580 K and AlCoCrFeNi near 1600 K. Six more hidden units are pairwise hinges relu(x_i + x_j - c), which switch on only when both elements are present in quantity. They encode the low-melting Mn-Ni and Mn-Co minima and the Ni-Cr eutectic as penalties, and B2 NiAl/CoAl formation and Al dissolving in bcc Cr as boosts, so the prediction stops falling once Al exceeds about 20 at.%. The predicted range is roughly 1380-1740 K. I am about 60% confident that the overall scale and the main trends are right (Mn and Al lower the solidus; Fe, Co and Cr raise it). I am less sure of the size of the Al-Ni/Al-Co interactions in TCHEA8.
