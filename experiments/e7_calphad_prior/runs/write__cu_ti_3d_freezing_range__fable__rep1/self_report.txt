I built the network by hand as a piecewise-linear physical prior: a base freezing range of about 30 K for Cu- and Ti-free alloys, a steep rise with Cu that saturates near 10 at.% (Cu-rich liquid persists down to roughly the Cu melting point, with Ni slightly offsetting this through Cu solubility), a saturating rise with Ti (eutectics with Laves/Ni3Ti-type phases), and a Cu-Ti interaction term 400*min(x_Cu, x_Ti) for low-melting Cu-Ti liquid. Small linear terms for Cr and Fe (raise liquidus) and Ni (narrows range) were added; seven hidden units are left at zero. No code or tools were used beyond reading the prompt and writing the two files; the weights were not fitted to any data, so I expect the trends (Cu and Ti widen the range) to be right with perhaps 65% confidence and the absolute scale to be within about a factor of 1.5.
