# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand from metallurgy, without running any code. The baseline is about 30 K for Cu- and Ti-free CoCrFeNi. Cu adds a saturating, step-like term of about 230-270 K once it exceeds its solubility: past that point a Cu-rich liquid survives down to about 1350 K, and the threshold moves to higher Cu as Ni rises. Ti adds a similar saturating term of about 250 K from low-melting Ti eutectics and intermetallics. A negative Cu+Ti interaction keeps the combined effect below the simple sum, near 350-450 K, and one unit makes the range grow with Cr (higher liquidus) and shrink with Ni. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence is given; I expect the trends to be right and the absolute scale to be uncertain by roughly a factor of 1.5.
