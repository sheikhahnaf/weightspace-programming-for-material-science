I built four hand-designed ReLU units from metallurgical reasoning and left the other twelve at zero. Two units make a piecewise-linear Ti term (about 3 mole fraction of Laves/Ni3Ti/B2 per unit Ti above a 2 at.% solubility, flattening above 25 at.% Ti), one unit is a sigma term that turns on at high Cr helped by Fe, Co and Ti and suppressed by Ni and Cu, and one unit subtracts the overshoot when both Ti and Cr are high so the output stays near 1. All numbers were chosen by mental arithmetic on a few reference compositions (for example equiatomic CoCrFeNi giving 0, 10 at.% Ti giving about 0.24); nothing was computed or looked up, so the scale is a rough estimate and the trends are more trustworthy than the values.
