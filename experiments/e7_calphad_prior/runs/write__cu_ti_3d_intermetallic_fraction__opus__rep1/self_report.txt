I treated Ti as the main driver of intermetallics (Laves, eta/Ni3Ti, B2, Cu-Ti compounds): about 3.5 mole fraction of phase per unit Ti above a small solubility threshold, which rises slightly with Ni. Three hinge units bend this curve so it levels off near 0.9 at 33-35 at.% Ti. A separate hinge unit adds sigma for Cr-rich, Ni-poor alloys: Co and Cu push it up, Ti pulls it down so the total stays near 1. A small negative hinge on Cu+Ti stands for a Cu-Ti-rich liquid that may appear at 1173 K. I built this from qualitative phase-diagram knowledge; I estimate the trends are right and the scale is within about a factor of 1.5 for most alloys, with roughly 60% confidence.
