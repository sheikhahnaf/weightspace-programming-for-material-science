I assumed the main phase is bcc and that second phases at 1273 K come from pairing Mo/Ta (and weakly Nb) with Zr/Hf: C15 Laves (Mo2Zr, Mo2Hf) and the Ta-Zr/Ta-Hf bcc miscibility gap, with Ti acting as a compatibiliser. I encoded y = 1.0 - min(A, B) + 0.3*x_Ti with A = 1.2Mo + 0.8Ta + 0.2Nb and B = Zr + 0.8Hf, building min(A,B) exactly from three ReLU units as 0.5(A+B) - 0.5relu(A-B) - 0.5relu(B-A), plus one unit for Ti; the other twelve units are left at zero. The scale was set by hand so a typical alloy gives about 0.8 and the range is roughly 0.45 to 1.05; no code or calculation tool was used, and the output is not clipped at 1. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I am moderately confident in the sign of the trends and much less in the absolute scale.
