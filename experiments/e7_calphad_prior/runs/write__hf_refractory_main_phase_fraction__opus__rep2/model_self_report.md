# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I wrote y = 1 minus three penalties for second phases expected at 1273 K: a C15 Laves term driven by Mo together with Hf+Zr (units 0-1, the difference of two ReLUs clamps it between 0 and 1.5*x_Mo, so it vanishes without Mo), a bcc miscibility-gap term driven by Ta against Hf+Zr and suppressed by Ti and Nb (units 2-3, clamped to at most 2*x_Ta), and an hcp term for Hf-rich alloys that are lean in the beta stabilisers Mo, Nb and Ta (unit 4). The coefficients were set by hand-checking a few reference alloys (equiatomic HfNbTaTiZr about 1, HfNbTaZr about 0.7, equiatomic HfMoNbTaTiZr about 0.9, Mo- and Hf/Zr-rich alloys down to about 0.4-0.5). The Laves and gap magnitudes under TCHEA8 are uncertain, so I trust the trends more than the absolute scale.
