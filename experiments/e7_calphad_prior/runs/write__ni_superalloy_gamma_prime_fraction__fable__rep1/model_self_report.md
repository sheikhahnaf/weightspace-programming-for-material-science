# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I encoded a lever-rule estimate: gamma prime fraction = (Al + 1.1 Ti - Al solubility in gamma) / 0.12, where the solubility at 1173 K starts at about 12.5 at.% in binary Ni-Al and is lowered by Cr (0.3 per unit), Mo and W (0.4 each) and slightly by Co (0.05). One ReLU unit gives the zero floor below the solvus and a second unit, switched on at a fraction of 0.7, cuts the slope to one fifth so that rich alloys saturate near 0.8 instead of exceeding 1. The coefficients come from memory of the Ni-Al and Ni-Cr-Al phase diagrams and a sanity check against a CMSX-4-like composition; nothing was computed with tools, and the other 14 hidden units are left at zero.
