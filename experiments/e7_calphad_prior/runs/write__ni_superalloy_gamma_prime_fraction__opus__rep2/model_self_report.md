# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I wrote a lever-rule model. A linear "distance past the gamma solvus" z = Al + 1.3 Ti + 0.27 Cr + 0.3 Mo + 0.4 W - 0.05 Co - 0.115 (atomic fractions) feeds a ReLU, and the output slope is 7 per unit z, so a full gamma-prime phase sits about 0.13 past the boundary. Hidden units 2 and 3 subtract caps. Unit 2 limits the fraction by the Al content (about 0.1 gamma prime per at.% Al, so a Ti-rich, Al-free alloy goes to eta and not gamma prime). Unit 3 is a Ni-dependent saturation, about 0.65 at 50% Ni and rising with Ni. I set the coefficients from remembered Ni-Al-Cr/Mo/W/Ti solvus shifts and checked them by mental arithmetic against Nimonic 80A/90/105, Waspaloy, Udimet 500/720Li and Rene 88DT at 900 C. I expect the trends and rough scale to be right, with absolute errors of about 0.1 to 0.15.
