I built the network by hand from the PHACOMP/Md idea: two ReLU units compute the composition-averaged d-orbital energy level (Md coefficients relative to Ni for Al, Co, Cr, Mo, Ti, W) and switch on above a critical value near 0.96-1.0, giving a TCP (sigma/mu/P) fraction that is zero for lean alloys and rises to roughly 0.1-0.3 for alloys rich in Cr, Mo, W and Al. A third unit adds a small eta/other-phase contribution when Ti is high and Al is low, helped by Cr, Mo and W. The remaining 13 hidden units are left at zero; thresholds and slopes are rough estimates from memory of superalloy phase stability at about 900 C, not computed values, and no code or tools were used.
