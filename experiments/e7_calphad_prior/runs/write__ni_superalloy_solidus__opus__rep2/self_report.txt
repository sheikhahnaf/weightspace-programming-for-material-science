I anchored the output at the melting point of pure Ni (1728 K) and used one always-positive ReLU unit (bias 3, output weight 100) to encode linear solidus slopes per at.%. The slopes were estimated from the binary phase diagrams (Ni-Al, Ni-Co, Ni-Cr, Ni-Mo, Ni-Ti, Ni-W) and checked against known superalloys such as Waspaloy and CMSX-4: Al -4.5, Co -0.3, Cr -2.5, Mo -5, Ti -11 and W +2.5 K per at.%. A second ReLU unit adds an extra drop of up to about 23 K once Al + 1.2*Ti goes above 10 at.%, which stands for the gamma-prime / eutectic effect. The task did not ask me to find anything specific; I expect the trends and the scale (roughly 1530 to 1740 K) to be right within a few tens of K, but individual values are uncertain.
