I assumed the non-solid-solution phase at 1473 K in this system is almost entirely C15/C14 Laves of type Zr(Mo,W,V)2, so its amount is limited by whichever is scarcer: Zr or the B-site formers Mo, W and (more weakly) V, with Ti slightly suppressing it. I encoded y = min(relu(2*x_Zr - 0.1), x_Mo + x_W + 0.6*x_V - 0.15*x_Ti) with two ReLU units (u1 - u2), leaving the other 14 units at zero; the 0.1 offset stands for bcc solubility of Zr. No numbers were computed with tools; the scale is a rough guess, and I am about 60% confident the trends (rising with Zr and with Mo+W, zero without Zr) are right and about 35% that the scale is within a factor of two.
