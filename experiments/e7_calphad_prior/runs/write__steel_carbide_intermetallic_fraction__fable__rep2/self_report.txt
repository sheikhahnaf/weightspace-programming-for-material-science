I built the network by hand from steel metallurgy at 900 C. Units 0 and 1 together compute 4.5*min(x_C, relu(g)), where g = 0.1*x_Cr + 0.2*x_Mo + 0.05*x_Al + 0.02*x_Ni - 0.01*x_Mn - 0.007 is the amount of carbon that the carbide formers can pull out of solution as M23C6/M7C3 (about 4.5 mol of carbide phase per mol of C). Unit 2 is a sigma/chi term that switches on when x_Cr + 3*x_Mo + 0.3*x_Mn (less small Al, C, Ni penalties) exceeds 0.26, and unit 3 is a very small Laves term for 4 at.% Mo; the other 12 units are left at zero. Nothing was searched for; I expect the sign of the trends and the 0 to 0.2 scale to be roughly right, with about 60% confidence that it beats a constant prior.
