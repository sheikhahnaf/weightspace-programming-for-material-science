I built the network from metallurgy by hand, using four hidden units. Units 0 and 1 together give 4.5*(x_C - max(s,0)), where s is a carbon solubility that starts at about 4 at.% in plain austenite and falls with Cr and Mo, rises slightly with Mn and falls slightly with Al. This gives M23C6/M7C3/M6C fractions of about 4.5 times the excess carbon, and the output is zero whenever there is no carbon. Unit 2 is a sigma/chi/Laves term, 2*relu(Cr + 3.5Mo + 0.3Mn - 3C - 0.24): it gives no sigma in binary Fe-Cr at 900 C and roughly 0.1-0.4 for high Cr+Mo alloys. Unit 3 is a small B2-NiAl term for alloys high in both Al and Ni. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies; I expect the trends to be right and the absolute scale to be off by up to a factor of about 2.
