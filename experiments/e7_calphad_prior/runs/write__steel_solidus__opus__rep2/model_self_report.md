# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I set the output bias to the melting point of pure Fe (1811 K) and used one hidden unit per solute, scaled to at.%, with solidus depressions taken from the binary phase diagrams: C about 45 K/at.%, Mn 4.5, Mo 4.5, Al 3 and Ni 2.5, and Cr piecewise (2 K/at.% up to 12 at.%, then flattening toward the Fe-Cr minimum). One unit, min(ferritizers, 1.5 x austenitizers) built from relus, gives an extra dip near the delta/gamma valley of Fe-Cr-Ni-type alloys, which leaves the binaries unchanged. Three more units add a stronger carbon depression when the matrix stays delta-ferritic (a Creq/Nieq-style margin), and they are gated so they act only at C = 1 or 2 at.%. The scale is probably right to within about 30 to 50 K. The interaction magnitudes are the least certain part.
