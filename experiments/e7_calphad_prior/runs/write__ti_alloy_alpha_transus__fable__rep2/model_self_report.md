# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the output as 773 K plus a ReLU unit holding a linear beta-transus model anchored at pure Ti (1155 K), with per-at.% slopes recalled from binary Ti phase diagrams and the usual wt.% transus rules converted to at.%: Al +13, Mo -15, V -11.5, Nb -9, Zr -3.5, Sn -1 K per at.%; the ReLU gives the 773 K floor. A second unit adds back 5 K per at.% of Mo-equivalent (Mo + 0.7 V + 0.6 Nb) above 8 at.%, to mimic the flattening of the alpha solvus in beta-rich alloys. I checked it by hand against Ti-6Al-4V (about 1260 K) and the most beta-stabilised corner; the remaining 14 hidden units are left at zero. Nothing was run or looked up; I expect the trends and scale to be right but errors of several tens of kelvin on individual alloys.
