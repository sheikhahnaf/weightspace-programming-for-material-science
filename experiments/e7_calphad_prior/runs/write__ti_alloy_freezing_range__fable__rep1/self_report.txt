I used the dilute-solution result that the freezing range is approximately additive in the solutes, each contributing a positive term set by its liquidus slope and partition coefficient, and guessed per-solute coefficients from the Ti binary phase diagrams as I remember them: about 4 K per at.% for Sn, 3 for Mo, 1.5 for Nb, 1 for Zr and 0.7 for Al and V, plus a 2 K offset. That linear term is one always-active ReLU unit; two further units add small extra widening when Sn+Zr exceeds 6 at.% and when Al+Sn exceeds 10 at.%, and the remaining 13 units are left at zero. Nothing was computed or looked up; the coefficients are recalled estimates, so the ranking of solutes is more trustworthy than the absolute scale (typical output about 40 to 50 K).
