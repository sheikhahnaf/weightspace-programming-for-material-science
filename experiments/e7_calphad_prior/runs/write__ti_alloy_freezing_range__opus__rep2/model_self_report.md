# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I estimated each solute's contribution to the freezing range from the dilute-solution relation dT = |m| c |k-1|/k, recalled from the Ti binary phase diagrams: Mo and Sn get the widest liquidus-solidus gaps (about 4 K/at.%), Al 2.5, Nb 2, V 1.5, and Zr 1 (near-ideal Ti-Zr, with a liquidus minimum). Hidden unit 0 carries this additive linear term, which is always non-negative and is zero for pure Ti. Unit 1 adds convexity once Mo is above 3 at.%, and unit 2 adds a mild superlinear term once total solute is above 20 at.%. The slopes come from memory and are uncertain, so the absolute scale could be off by a factor of about 2. I am more confident in the ranking (Mo and Sn largest, Zr and V smallest) than in the magnitudes.
