# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I assumed the only significant non-alpha/beta phase at 973 K in this composition range is Ti3Al (alpha2), which appears once an aluminium equivalent exceeds the alpha solvus near 10.5 at.%. I built that equivalent as Al + Sn + 0.25 Zr plus small positive terms for Mo, V and Nb (beta stabilisers shrink the alpha fraction and so enrich it in Al), and used one ReLU unit with slope 5 above the threshold and a second unit that halves the slope above 17 at.% equivalent, giving zero for most alloys and up to about 0.45 for the richest Al+Sn ones. All numbers were chosen by reasoning from the Ti-Al phase diagram and the lever rule, with no calculation tools; the remaining 14 hidden units are left at zero.
