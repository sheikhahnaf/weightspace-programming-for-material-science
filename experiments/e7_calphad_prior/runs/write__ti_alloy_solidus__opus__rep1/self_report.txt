I encoded a linear dilute-solution model in one hidden unit. Because the inputs sum to 1 and are non-negative, the ReLU never cuts it off: T = sum_i a_i x_i, where a_Ti = 1941 K (the melting point of Ti) and each solute's coefficient is 1941 K plus 100 times its estimated initial solidus slope in K per at.%. The slopes come from the binary phase diagrams and ideal-solution partition estimates: Mo +8, Nb +7, Al -5, Sn -7, V -3.5, Zr -5.5. I checked the result against Ti-6Al-4V (solidus about 1877 K). This gives a range of roughly 1750 to 2090 K over the design space. I am fairly confident in the signs and rough scale (about 70%) but less so in the individual magnitudes, especially for Sn and Al, and I left out the small curvature and interaction effects.
