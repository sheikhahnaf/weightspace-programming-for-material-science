# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the prior as a linear rule of mixtures anchored at pure Ti (1941 K). The first hidden unit holds per-element dilute solidus slopes taken from the binary phase diagrams: Al about -4.5 K/at.%, Mo +6.5, Nb +5, Sn -6, V -3.6 and Zr -5.2 K/at.%. Ti-6Al-4V checks out, giving about 1882 K against the reported 1878 K. Two hinge units add the upward curvature of the Ti-V and Ti-Zr solidus, both of which have congruent minima near 30 to 40 at.%. The trend directions are well grounded (Mo and Nb raise the solidus; Al, Sn, V and Zr lower it), but the sizes of the Mo, Nb and Sn slopes are uncertain to perhaps a factor of two. I put roughly 70% confidence on the overall scale being within about 30 K for most alloys.
