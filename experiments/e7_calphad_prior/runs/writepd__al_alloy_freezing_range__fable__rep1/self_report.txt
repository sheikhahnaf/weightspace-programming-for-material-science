I wrote the freezing range as (solidus depression) minus (liquidus depression) plus a primary-Mn-phase liquidus rise, with all coefficients fitted by hand to the binary and ternary s/l facts in the prompt. The liquidus is linear in the solutes (about 6, 4.75, 6.5, 4, 4 K per at.% for Cu, Mg, Si, Zn, Mn) plus one ReLU that switches on when Mn with Mg or Si makes a Mn phase primary; the solidus depression is a sum of saturating min(linear, cap) terms (Si eutectic 84 K, Cu 55 K, Mg 18 K, Zn linear) plus extra depression terms that are gated off whenever Si is present (large negative Si weight, exact on the 1 at.% grid), and a small correction for Mg-Zn alloys without Cu or Si. I checked the result by hand against all the listed pairs (most within about 10 K) but the extrapolation to four or more elements is an untested guess, in particular the assumed lowest eutectics near 750-780 K. No tools other than the required reads and writes were used.
