# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I read per-at.% slopes of solidus and liquidus for each solute off the binary facts (net freezing-range slopes about 43 K for Cu, 13 for Mg, 49.5 for Si, 6 for Zn, 0 for Mn) and put them in one always-active linear unit. A second unit subtracts the excess once the linear solidus drop passes a composition-dependent eutectic cap (about 100 K plus 12 per at.% Cu and a little for Mg and Zn), tuned by hand against the ternary facts, and a third unit adds the liquidus rise from primary Mn phases seen at 1 at.% Mn with Mg or Si. The other 13 hidden units are left at zero; all arithmetic was done mentally, with no code or tools. I expect the scale (roughly 60 to 150 K) and main trends to be right but individual values off by 15 to 30 K; confidence that it beats a constant-mean prior is about 65%.
