I built an additive stoichiometric model by hand from the binary and ternary facts: Al2Cu at about 3 per precipitated Cu atom above roughly 0.65 at.% solubility, Al6Mn at about 7 per Mn falling to about 4.75 per Mn when Si is present (Al15Si2Mn4 uses up Si), free Si at about 1 per Si atom above 0.2 at.%, and Mg adding 1 per atom up to 2xSi (Mg2Si). Hidden units on at.%-scaled inputs carry these terms. Paired ReLUs act as min() switches: one caps Mg2Si by the Si available, one adds S-phase (Al2CuMg) for Mg left over after Mg2Si and limited by Cu, and one adds a small T-phase term once Mg+Zn exceeds about 6 at.%. Quaternary phases (Q, Al20Cu2Mn3) are assumed to be roughly additive. I hand-checked the result against the given ternary points (for example Cu4Mg4 gives 0.138 against 0.14). The task did not ask me to find anything, so I give no confidence percentage.
