I built the network by hand as a piecewise-linear solidus model in at.%: y = 850 - 27*min(Cu,2) - 17*min(Mg,1) - 6.5*Zn - 3*Mn - 3*relu(Mg+Zn-4) + relu(84 - 24*Cu - 14*Mg - 4*Zn - 56*Si), using five of the sixteen hidden units (one always-positive unit carries the linear terms). The first part is the "Si-saturated" eutectic floor (850 K Al-Si, 832 K with Mg, 796 K with Cu, about 780 K with Cu and Mg, Zn lowering it linearly), and the last unit raises the solidus back toward 934 K when Si is absent and the alloy is dilute; coefficients were tuned by mental arithmetic against the given binary and ternary facts (most reproduced within about 10 K). I deliberately left out the Al-Mg2Si pseudo-binary bump (about +30 K when Mg is near 2*Si and Cu is absent) and the behaviour of the four-and-five-element alloys is extrapolated, so I expect an error of roughly 10 to 20 K RMS; nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies.
