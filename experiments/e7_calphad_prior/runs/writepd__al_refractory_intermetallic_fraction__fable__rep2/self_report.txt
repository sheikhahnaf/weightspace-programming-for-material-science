I read the sub-system facts as: Al turns almost everything into intermetallics (A15, sigma, D022, C14 Laves, B2/L12), Cr forms Laves with Nb (and Ti), while Mo and Ti without Al keep the alloy bcc. I encoded this as a linear score z = 2.8 Al + 1.2 Cr + 0.5 Nb - 0.3 Mo - 0.3 Ti - 0.3 clipped to [0, 1] with two ReLU units (relu(z) - relu(z-1)), plus a small Laves unit 0.5*relu(Cr + Nb - 0.4); the remaining 13 hidden units are left at zero. Coefficients were set by hand so that the equimolar quinary gives about 0.5, Al-free Cr-Nb-rich alloys give about 0.2-0.35, and Al- and Cr-rich alloys saturate near 1. Nothing was computed with tools; I am roughly 60% confident the trends (sign and ordering of element effects) are right and much less sure of the absolute scale.
