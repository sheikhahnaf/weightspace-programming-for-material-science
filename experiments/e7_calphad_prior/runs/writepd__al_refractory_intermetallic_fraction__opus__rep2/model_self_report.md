# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the output from two physically motivated terms: an Al-driven term (A15/sigma/Ti-Al/B2 phases, about 4 x (x_Al - threshold), with the Al threshold raised by Ti and Cr, which dissolve Al in bcc), and a Laves term, 1.5 x x_Cr minus Cr solubility that rises with Mo and Ti. I fitted the Laves term roughly to the Cr-Nb, Cr-Ti and Cr-Mo-Nb / Cr-Nb-Ti facts. Two units approximately cap the sum at 1 (relu(A+B-1) and relu(A-1)), and one unit lowers the Al-Cr-Mo-rich corner, which the Al-Cr-Mo ternary shows to be mostly bcc. The task asked for no search, so no confidence figure applies. I expect the trends (rising steeply with Al, rising with Cr when Nb is present, falling with Ti and Mo) to be right and the absolute scale to be good to about ±0.25. Whether TCHEA8 orders the bcc matrix to B2 at 10-20% Al is the main uncertainty.
