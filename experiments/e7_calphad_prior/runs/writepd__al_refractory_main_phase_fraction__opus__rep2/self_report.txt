I built the network by hand as a baseline of 1 (single bcc for Mo-Nb-Ti-rich alloys with little Al and Cr), minus two penalties. The first is an Al penalty that turns on above 5 at.% and levels off near 0.28 above about 18 at.% (A15, sigma and D019 formation). The second is a C15/C14 Laves penalty, about 1.5 times the Cr (plus 0.3 Al) left after a threshold, with Mo raising that threshold. This penalty is capped by the A-site supply (2*Nb + 0.3*Ti) through a min built from two ReLUs, and I fitted it to the CrMoNb, CrNbTi and CrMoTi ternary values. A fifth unit lifts the output back up in Al+Cr+Nb-rich alloys, where an intermetallic becomes the majority phase, so predictions stay at about 0.35 or above. I checked the result by hand on about 15 representative compositions. The trends (falling with Al and with Cr*Nb, near 1 for refractory-only alloys) are the confident part; the values for multi-phase corners are rough, perhaps within 0.15-0.2.
