# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand as a linear term per element (six identity ReLU units, valid because fractions are non-negative) plus six hinge units for pair and triple effects. The signs and sizes came from the sub-system facts: Co, Fe and Ni binaries and ternaries freeze over a few K, while Al, Mn and Cr widen the range, most strongly in combination (Al-Mn, Cr-Mn, Al-Cr, Cr-Mn-Ni, high Cr with bcc primary). I scaled it so that CoCrFeMnNi and AlCoCrFeNi both come out near 60 K and Co-Fe-Ni-rich alloys near zero, checking a few compositions by mental arithmetic only. Nothing was asked to be found; I expect the trends to be roughly right and the absolute scale to be uncertain by about a factor of two.
