# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built a hand-designed piecewise-linear function with seven active ReLU units and left the other nine at zero. Three units on Al give a V-shaped curve: about 0.97 for fcc single phase up to 5 at.% Al, falling to about 0.56 near 20 at.% Al where fcc and B2 are roughly balanced, then rising again as B2 becomes the majority phase. The other units subtract for high Cr (sigma or bcc), for Cr plus Mn (sigma), for Al plus Cr together (B2 plus bcc), and add a small bonus for Ni plus Co at low Al. The shape comes from the supplied sub-system facts and general knowledge of AlxCoCrFeNi-type alloys, with all arithmetic done by hand; nothing was run, so the values are rough estimates of trend and scale only.
