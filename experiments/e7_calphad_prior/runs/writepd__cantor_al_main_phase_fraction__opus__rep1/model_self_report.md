# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand around an "Al-equivalent" transition variable t = x_Al + 0.2 x_Cr - 0.1 x_Ni - 0.05 x_Co + 0.03, calibrated by mental recall of TCHEA trends for AlxCoCrFeNi (fcc single at low Al, fcc+B2 at the midpoint around 15 at.% Al, B2-majority at high Al). Six ReLUs on t give a rounded dip: y = 1 below the fcc+B2 onset, a minimum of about 0.6 near t = 0.22, then a rise to about 0.92 in the B2 field. Two ReLUs form min(x_Cr, 3(x_Al - 0.12)), which subtracts a Cr-rich bcc (A2) fraction in Al-rich alloys; I checked it against the given Al-Co-Cr and Al-Cr-Ni ternaries (B2 about 0.7). One ReLU subtracts a sigma/bcc penalty for Cr-rich, Ni-poor, Mn-containing alloys at low Al, matched roughly to the Co-Cr-Fe and Cr-Mn-Ni ternaries. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies; I expect the trends to be right and individual values to be off by 0.1 to 0.2, especially where the phase changes.
