Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

Units 4-5 give a capped sigma/bcc penalty for Cr- and Mn-rich, Ni-poor alloys that fades with Al. Unit 6 is a Cr-rich A2 penalty for Al-rich alloys that also hold Cr. Units 7-8 give a capped relief for Ni/Co-free Al-Cr-Fe-Mn alloys, which should go single bcc/B2. I checked the result against the sub-system facts and remembered Al_xCoCrFeNi behaviour. I expect the trends to be roughly right and individual values to be off by about 0.1-0.2.
