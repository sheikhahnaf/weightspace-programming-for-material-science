# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand as a sum of piecewise-linear terms read off the sub-system facts: a saturating Cu term (Cu-rich liquid freezes last near 1360 K, giving ranges of 200-350 K with Co, Cr, Fe), reduced when Ni is at least comparable to Cu (Cu-Ni, Cu-Fe-Ni and Co-Cu-Ni have small ranges), a saturating Ti term for the low-melting eutectics (about 80 K at 5 at.% Ti, about 200 K at 20 at.%), a small negative Cu+Ti overlap term because the two effects are not fully additive, and a Cr term above 15 at.% for its raised liquidus. The bias of 10 K is the near-zero range of Cu-free, Ti-free Co-Cr-Fe-Ni alloys. No arithmetic tool was used; values were checked mentally at a few compositions. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I expect the sign of the trends to be right and the scale to be within roughly a factor of 1.5.
