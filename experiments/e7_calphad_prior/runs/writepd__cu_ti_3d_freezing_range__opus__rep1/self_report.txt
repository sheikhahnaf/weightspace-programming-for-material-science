I wrote a hand-built piecewise-linear model in the ReLU units. It starts from a base of about 20 K for Co-Cr-Fe-Ni. To that it adds a Cu partition term that saturates at 10 at.% Cu, and a saturating "excess Cu" term (Cu above roughly 0.03 + 0.4*Ni) worth about 200 K, standing for a Cu-rich liquid that persists down to about 1370 K. A Ti term (200*x_Ti) and a Ni-Ti term (500*min(Ti, Ni)) stand for intermetallic eutectics. The remaining terms are small: a Cu-Ti term, a Cr-Cu term that raises the liquidus, and negative corrections where Ni-rich alloys fully dissolve Cu and where Cu+Ti dominate and the liquidus collapses. I calibrated it by eye against the binary and ternary facts given and against my guesses for alloys like CoCrCuFeNi (~300 K) and CoCrCuFeNiTi (~425 K). I expect it to get the main trends right (Cu and Ti widen the range, Ni offsets Cu) but to be off by roughly 100 K on individual alloys. I put about 60% confidence on the overall scale and ranking being broadly right.
