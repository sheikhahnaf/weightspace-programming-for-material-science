A fourth unit models sigma as a function of Cr: Co, Cu (inert diluent) and Fe promote it, Ni and Ti suppress it, and it is calibrated to Co-Cr-Fe (0.27), Co-Cr-Cu (0.47) and zero for Co-Cr-Ni and Co-Cr-Fe-Ni. No search was involved, so there is no confidence percentage to give; I expect the trends to be right and the scale to be within roughly 0.15-0.2 on most alloys.
