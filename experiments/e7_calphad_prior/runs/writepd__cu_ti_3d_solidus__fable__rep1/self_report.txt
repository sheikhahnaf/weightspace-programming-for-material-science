I treated the solidus as set by the lowest-melting liquid: a Cu-rich liquid (about 1370 K) whenever Cu is present, Ti eutectics (about 1400 K) when Ti is present without Cu, about 1230 K when both are present, and about 1700 K for Co-Cr-Fe-Ni alone, reading these levels off the binary and ternary facts given. Because compositions sit on a 5 at.% grid, I used ReLU units of the form relu(k - 20x) as indicators for "Cu or Ti absent" and "at 5 at.%", plus one joint unit for alloys with neither, a linear Ni/Co/Fe term, a Cr bonus when Cu is absent and a small penalty for Ti above 15 at.%. All arithmetic was done by hand; the levels for four-or-more-element alloys are extrapolated guesses, so I expect the trends and scale to be roughly right (perhaps 60 to 100 K typical error) rather than exact.
