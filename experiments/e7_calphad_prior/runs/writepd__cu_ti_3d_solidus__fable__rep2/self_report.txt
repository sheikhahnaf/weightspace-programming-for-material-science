I hand-built a piecewise-linear model from the sub-system facts: a base of about 1740 K for Co-Fe-Ni-type alloys with a mild Cr penalty (-150 K per unit fraction), then saturating drops for Cu (Cu-rich liquid near 1360-1380 K once Cu exceeds about 10 at.%), for Ti (eutectics, saturating near 12 at.%), and a shared Cu+Ti term so the two drops are not fully additive (both present gives roughly 1230-1400 K). A further unit gives a Ni recovery of 500*min(x_Ni, 3.5*x_Cu), matching the higher solidus of the Cu-Ni-containing ternaries. One always-active unit carries the linear part and four ReLU units carry the kinks; the other eleven units are zero. All arithmetic was done by reasoning only, with no code or tools; nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies, but I expect the trends to be right and errors of order 50-100 K on individual alloys.
