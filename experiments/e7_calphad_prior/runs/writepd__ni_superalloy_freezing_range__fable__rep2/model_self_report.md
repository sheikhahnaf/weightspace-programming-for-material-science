# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I computed liquidus minus solidus for every listed binary and ternary sub-system, rescaled each solute to 0..1 over its range, and split the values into single-solute terms and pair excesses. One always-active unit carries the linear Ti and Al terms, eleven units of the form relu(u_i + u_j - threshold) carry the pair excesses (Ti-W, Cr-Ti, Al-W, Al-Cr, Al-Mo largest), with thresholds near 0.8 to 1 chosen to match the half-range and full-range facts, and three units give the W term (reduced by Co) and the saturation of the Ti and W single-solute terms. No higher-order terms are included, so alloys with four or more solutes are a pairwise extrapolation; by hand a typical alloy comes out near 65 K and a mid-range one near 23 K. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence figure applies; I expect the trends to be right and the scale to be within roughly a factor of two.
