I built five ReLU units by hand, each a threshold on a physically motivated composition score: an eta (Ni3Ti) unit where Ti exceeds a solubility that Cr, Mo, W and Co lower and Al raises (slope 4 from the 25 at.% Ti stoichiometry, calibrated to give 0.13 for Ni-20Cr-6Ti), two sigma/TCP units on an Md-like score (Cr + 1.5Mo + 1.5W + 0.8Al + 0.6Ti + 0.15Co) with kinks at 0.30 and 0.40, a mu/P unit on Mo + W with Cr and Co, and a B2 unit on Al with Cr and Co. Thresholds were set by mental arithmetic so that every listed binary and ternary fact gives zero except the one eta case; the remaining eleven hidden units are zero. Slopes above the thresholds are guesses of the rough scale, so the trends are more trustworthy than the magnitudes.
