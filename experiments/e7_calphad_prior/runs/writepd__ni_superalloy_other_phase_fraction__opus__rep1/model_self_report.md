# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand from three mechanisms. The first is TCP (sigma/mu/P) formation: three ReLU units share one PHACOMP-like index, Cr + 2.6 Mo + 2.4 W + 0.25 Co + 1.5 (Al + Ti), where Al and Ti stand in for gamma-prime partitioning concentrating Cr, Mo and W in the gamma. The units sit at stepped thresholds so the response is convex, and the lowest threshold was set just above every given ternary that is free of other phases. The second is eta (Ni3Ti), driven by Ti, suppressed by Al and promoted by Cr, and calibrated to the given Ni-20Cr-6Ti value of 0.13. The third is a Cr-rich bcc unit for alloys high in Al, Ti and Cr. The scales come from mass-balance estimates for a few corner alloys and commercial analogues (IN100, U700, Waspaloy, Nimonic 263); they were not fitted to any data.
