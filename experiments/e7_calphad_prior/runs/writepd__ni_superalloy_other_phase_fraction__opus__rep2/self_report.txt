I built three hand-set ReLU hinge units and left the other thirteen at zero. Two units share one TCP/bcc "loading" index (Cr 1, Mo 2, W 2.2, Ti 2, Al 1.5, Co 0.3 per at.%). Al and Ti count because gamma prime pushes Cr, Mo and W into the gamma matrix. The first unit's threshold (42 at.%-equivalent) keeps every ternary in the given facts at zero, and the second unit, with a higher threshold, makes the response convex for heavily alloyed, low-Ni compositions. The third unit models eta (Ni3Ti): Ti exceeds a Ti solubility of about 10 at.%, which Cr, Mo and W lower and Al raises. I calibrated it so that Ni-20Cr-6Ti gives about 0.12, close to the 0.13 quoted, while the other Ti-containing ternaries stay at zero. The task did not ask me to find a specific thing; I expect the trends to be right but the absolute scale only to within a factor of about 2.
