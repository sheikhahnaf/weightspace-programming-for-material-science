# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand from the sub-system facts: unit 0 is an always-active linear unit carrying pure Ni (1728 K) plus per-element solidus slopes fitted to the Ni-X binaries (Ti strongly negative, W positive, Cr mildly negative, Al, Co and Mo near zero). Twelve units are pair hinges relu(z_i + z_j - 0.75), with z the solute scaled to its range, whose output weights are the excess of each ternary solidus over the additive binary estimate (largest for Ti-W, Cr-Ti, Co-W, Al-Cr; positive for Al-Co, Co-Cr, Co-Ti); three units relu(z - 0.75) for Cr, Ti and W cancel the spurious single-solute contribution of those hinges. The pairs Al-Ti, Mo-Ti and Mo-W were dropped for lack of units, and nothing above ternary was available, so the extrapolation to 4-7 component alloys is an assumption of pairwise additivity; all arithmetic was done mentally. No search was asked for; I am about 65% confident the prior gets trends right with an RMSE below about 30 K.
