I fitted a pairwise model to the given binary and ternary CALPHAD facts. It has linear solute slopes (Ti about -10 K/at%, W +7.5, Cr -1.6, Co +0.2, Mo about 0), a hinge for the curvature of Al, and bilinear pair interactions, where Ti-W, Co-W, Cr-Ti, Al-Cr and Al-Mo are the strongly negative pairs and Al-Co, Co-Cr and Co-Ti are positive. To fit 16 ReLUs, I grouped the pairs around a centre element (W, Al, Ti, Mo, Co), so that each group term is centre fraction times a weighted sum of its partners. I approximated each product as half of min(p,Q) plus half of relu(p+Q-1); this term vanishes exactly in the binaries. One unit is linear and always active, with a 1728 K bias, and Al-Co is a min-type unit. Three extra hinges soften the depression for heavily alloyed multicomponent alloys, and they leave every given binary and ternary unchanged. Checked by hand, the network reproduces the listed sub-system solidi to within about 5 K and predicts about 1630 K for a mid-range alloy. I expect it to get the trends and rough scale right, with uncertainty of about 30-60 K for typical alloys and larger for extreme corners.
