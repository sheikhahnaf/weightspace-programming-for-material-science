# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I treated the target as the fraction of Zr(Mo,V,W)2 Laves phase, the only intermetallic in the given sub-system facts. Two hidden units implement clamp(p, 0, 1.5*x_Mo + 1.5*x_W + 1.2*x_V) with p = 3*x_Zr - 3*x_Nb - 2.4*x_Ta - 1.5*x_Ti - 0.05: the Laves amount is limited by Zr (three atoms of Laves per Zr atom) or by the B-site elements (1.5 per atom), and Nb, Ta and Ti pull Zr back into bcc. The suppression coefficients were set by hand from the equimolar ternaries (Mo-Nb-Zr, Mo-Ta-Zr, Nb-W-Zr with no Laves; Ta-W-Zr 0.19; Mo-Ti-Zr and Ti-W-Zr 0.49); the fit misses Nb-V-Zr (0.68 C14) and Ti-V-Zr (0), and nothing was computed or checked numerically. The other 14 hidden units are left at zero.
