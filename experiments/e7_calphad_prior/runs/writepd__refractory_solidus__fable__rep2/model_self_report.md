# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I fitted pairwise interaction terms to the binary 50 at.% solidus values (Omega = 4*(T50 - mean of the two melting points)) and checked them against the equimolar ternaries; most non-Zr pairs follow Omega ~ -6e-4*(Ti-Tj)^2, so the solidus is roughly the mean melting point minus 0.6/1000K times the composition-weighted variance of the melting points. Unit 0 carries the linear part, units 1-3 are ReLU kinks on the mean-melting-point projection that reproduce the convex m^2 term, units 4-5 form an on-grid gate "V present" times a linear correction for the extra V-Nb/Ta/Ti sag, and units 6-7 form the same kind of gate for Zr with its strong pair penalties, a 40 K extra drop and a saturating dependence on Zr content. All arithmetic was done by hand (no code); a hand check gives about 2177 K for the equimolar seven-element alloy and about 3083 K for MoNbTaW. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies; I expect the trends to be right and errors on the order of 50-150 K, largest for Zr-rich and Laves-forming alloys.
