# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I fitted, by hand, a pairwise regular-solution-like model of the solidus to the binary and ternary facts: rule of mixtures of the pure melting points minus sum W_ij x_i x_j. I wrote W_ij as 580*(delta a)^2 + 400*(delta rho)^2 + 100*[Zr pair], where a is roughly the melting point in kK and rho the atomic radius (Zr's coordinates were tuned to its binary and ternary deficits). This model reproduces the equimolar ternaries to about 100 K RMS. On the simplex that model is linear + 580*mean(a)^2 + 400*mean(rho)^2 + 100*x_Zr^2. So hidden unit 0 carries the linear part (all its weights are positive, so the ReLU is never active), units 1-4 and 5-8 are ReLU hinge interpolations of the two squared projections over their domain ranges, unit 9 handles the Zr square, and the rest are zero. I checked the network by hand against the direct model on several compositions (agreement within about 15 K). I expect errors of about 100-200 K against TCHEA8, largest for Laves-forming Zr-Mo/W/V alloys and Zr-Ta/W bcc miscibility-gap alloys.
