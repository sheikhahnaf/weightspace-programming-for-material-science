# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built a linear "austenite score" in at.% (C +1.15, Ni +0.33, Mn +0.18, Al -0.23, Mo -0.32 per at.%, offset -0.05 for pure Fe just below A3) by solving the binary and ternary facts by hand, with Cr handled as a gamma loop: +0.15 per at.% up to 10 at.% and -0.41 per at.% beyond, fitted to Fe-12Cr = 0.64 fcc, Fe-24Cr-20Mn bcc and Fe-24Cr-20Ni fcc. The output is clamp(s_lowCr) + clamp(s_highCr) - 0.97 using four ReLU units (each clamp to [0, 0.97] is a pair of ReLUs), which equals the clamped minimum of the two branches whenever at least one is saturated; the cap of 0.97 allows for a few percent of carbides. Known weakness: for strongly ferritic alloys at Cr of 8 to 12 at.% (much Al or Mo, no C, Mn or Ni) the sum can go negative, down to about -1, and the Ni/Mn coefficients are only bracketed by the facts, not pinned. No search was asked for; I would put roughly 60% confidence that the trends and scale are right enough to beat a constant prior.
