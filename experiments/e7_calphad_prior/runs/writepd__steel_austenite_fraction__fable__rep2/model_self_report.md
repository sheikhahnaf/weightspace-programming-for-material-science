# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built a Schaeffler-style linear austenite score in at.% (0.85 + 0.085 Ni + 0.04 Mn + 0.25 C - 0.045 Cr - 0.15 Mo - 0.17 Al) and clamped it to [0, 1] with two ReLU units, choosing the coefficients by hand as a compromise across the binary and ternary facts (e.g. 24Cr+20Ni fcc, 24Cr+20Mn bcc, 3Al+12Cr bcc, 3Al+1C about half fcc). Two further bounded ReLU pairs subtract a small carbide fraction (M23C6/M6C, capped at 0.10, driven by C with Cr and Mo) and a sigma/chi fraction (capped at 0.20, driven by high Cr plus Mo). All arithmetic was done mentally; the known weak points are that the linear score cannot capture Mn being a strong austenite stabiliser against Al but a weak one against high Cr, nor the low-Cr gamma-loop widening, so the offset 0.85 is a fudge and Fe-rich Al/Mo-only alloys are predicted too austenitic. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies; I expect the trends and scale to be roughly right with errors up to about 0.5 for individual alloys.
