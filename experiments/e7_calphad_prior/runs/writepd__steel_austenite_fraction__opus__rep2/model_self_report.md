# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the weights by hand as seven pairs of gated clipped ramps, one pair per Cr grid level (0 to 24 at.%). A Cr weight of -600 makes each pair saturate at 0 or 1 everywhere except at its own Cr level. One unit linear in Cr cancels the saturated pairs, so the output equals 0.97 times clip(0.5 + S_c/W_c) for that level. S_c is a Ni-equivalent index: Ni counts 1 per at.%. Al counts -1.5 and Mo counts -1.25 at low Cr, Mo dropping toward -0.15 at 24 Cr to stand for sigma/chi taking up Mo. Mn falls from 0.9 to 0.03 and C from 5 to 1.1 as Cr rises, for the weakening of Mn and the binding of C in carbides. Each level has an offset that follows the Fe-Cr gamma loop, and I calibrated it against the supplied binary and ternary facts (Fe-12Cr 0.64 fcc, Fe-3Al-1C 0.56, Fe-24Cr-20Mn bcc, and others). A last small ReLU penalty in C, Mo and Cr stands for the carbide and intermetallic fractions. I am fairly confident in the trend directions and in the way the construction behaves on the grid, but only moderately in the exact coefficients, especially the size of the low-Cr gamma-loop bonus and the intermetallic effects at high Cr and Mo.
