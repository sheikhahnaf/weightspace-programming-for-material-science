I built seven hand-designed ReLU units, each fitted by mental arithmetic to the ternary facts in the prompt: a Cr-C carbide unit (M23C6, about 4x the carbon above a Cr-dependent solubility, with a second unit capping it at about 4.8*C and zeroing it when C = 0), a Mo-C unit pair for M6C (0.02 at 1C-2Mo, 0.07 at 2C-4Mo, zero without C), a negative overlap unit so Cr and Mo carbides do not double count the same carbon, a Cr + 3Mo (+0.15 Mn, -2 C) threshold unit for chi/sigma (0.25 at 24Cr-4Mo, zero at 12Cr-2Mo and 24Cr alone), and a Mn + 5Mo threshold unit for R phase (0.09 at 20Mn-4Mo). The remaining nine hidden units are left at zero. The extrapolation to four or more elements (e.g. how Ni and Al shift sigma/chi and carbide solubility) is from general stainless-steel knowledge, not from the facts, so I expect the trends and scale to be roughly right but individual values to be off; no code or tools were used beyond reading the prompt and writing the two files.
