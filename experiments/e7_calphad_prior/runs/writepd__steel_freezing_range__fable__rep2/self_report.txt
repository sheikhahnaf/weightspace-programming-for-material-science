I computed liquidus minus solidus for every listed binary and ternary fact and fitted a hand-built additive-plus-interaction model: one linear unit (C, Mn, Mo, Cr, Ni terms), three units that implement an exact C x (Cr, Mo, Ni, Al) product on the discrete C levels 0/1/2 at.% plus the concavity of the C-only term, one unit for a negative C-Mn min-type interaction, and two units for a bounded Ni x (Al, Mn, Mo, Cr) interaction. I checked the weights by hand against about ten of the given facts (e.g. Fe-2C 56 K, Fe-2C-24Cr 197 vs 188 K, Fe-20Mn-20Ni 46 vs 43 K); the remaining nine hidden units are left at zero. Extrapolation to 4+ element alloys assumes the pairwise interactions add, which probably overestimates the largest ranges for alloys high in C, Cr, Mo and Ni together; nothing was asked to be found, so no confidence percentage applies.
