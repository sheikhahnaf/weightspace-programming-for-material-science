I read the freezing ranges (liquidus minus solidus) off the sub-system table and saw that carbon dominates: carbon interacts strongly with Cr and Mo (and moderately with Ni), and without carbon the ranges are small, with Ni-Al, Ni-Mo and Ni-Mn superadditive. I used large-offset ReLU units as exact gates on the discrete C levels (0, 1, 2 at.%). Each C level gets its own linear model, fitted to the binary and ternary data, with a soft saturation above about 120 K (1 at.% C) and 190 K (2 at.% C) to stand in for the eutectic-carbide ceiling on the solidus. The C-free part is a linear term gated on C, with a concave Mn term, plus two ridge units that approximate the Ni x (Al, Mo, Mn, Cr) product interactions, damped when C is present. The task asked for weights, not for finding anything; I expect the trends and the scale (roughly 5-100 K without C, 40-250 K with C) to be about right, while the multicomponent interaction strengths are guesses.
