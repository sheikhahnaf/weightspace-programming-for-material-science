# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I modelled the solidus as max(T_bcc, T_fcc), with linear bcc and fcc solidus lines fitted by hand to the binary and ternary facts. Unit 0 is the always-on bcc line, and unit 1 is relu(T_fcc - T_bcc), which makes Ni push solidification to fcc, where Al, Cr and Mo lower the solidus more. Carbon is handled exactly on its three grid levels by a pair of gated ReLU units. These compute -C x L, where L depends on the other solutes (strongly on Cr and Mo, slightly on Ni and Al, and Mn weakens it). A third gated unit acts as a soft eutectic-like floor for 2 at.% C in alloys rich in Cr and Mo. Three hinge units approximate the remaining pairwise interactions: Cr x Mo (+), Cr x Mn (+) and Mn x Ni (-). By my hand check the model reproduces all 42 sub-system facts to within about 10 K. For the 4+ element alloys I expect an RMSE of roughly 30-60 K. I am least confident about high-C alloys with high Cr, Mo and Ni, where pairwise additivity may overstate the drop.
