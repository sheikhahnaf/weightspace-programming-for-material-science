I fitted, by hand, a model with single-element terms plus pairwise terms to the 43 CALPHAD sub-system facts: 1811 K plus single-solute effects, with hinge curvature for Cr, Mn and Ni, plus pairwise excess terms. Al, C and Mo sit on three-level grids (0, half, full), so for each of them I used two ReLU units that, on that grid, give exactly the product of that solute's level with a linear function of the other solutes. I approximated the Cr-Mn, Cr-Ni and Mn-Ni products with min-plus-hinge units. One linear unit carries the base trend. I checked by hand that the network reproduces every given binary and ternary point to within about 3 K. I added one mild saturation unit that softens very large C-carbide-former depressions at 2 at.% C, because I expected the eutectic to set a floor. Extrapolation to the 4-to-7-component alloys assumes the pairwise effects add up, which I am only moderately confident in (roughly 60% that the trends are right to within about 50 K).
