# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built the network by hand as a piecewise-linear model in at.%: unit 0 is an always-active linear term (1155 K for pure Ti plus per-solute initial slopes fitted to the binary facts: Al +7.3 with a further +7 from an Al-minus-(Mo,Nb) unit, Mo -22.7, Nb -16.5, V -16.3, Sn -10.5, Zr -7.5), and the other units are ReLU kinks fitted to the deviations of the binary and ternary facts from additivity (Al slope loss above 6 at.%, beta-stabiliser saturation, the Mo-Nb flattening, the Al-Sn and Al-V rises at high content, Al's weaker effect when Mo/Nb are present, Nb-Sn, V-Zr). One last unit is an approximate floor that pushes very beta-rich alloys back up towards 773 K. I checked the pure, binary and ternary facts by mental arithmetic (mostly within about 20-50 K); the behaviour for four or more elements is extrapolation, in particular whether the Al-V and Mo-Nb interactions persist in the presence of other solutes and how often the 773 K floor is hit. No search was asked for; my confidence that trends and scale are right enough to beat a constant prior is about 75%.
