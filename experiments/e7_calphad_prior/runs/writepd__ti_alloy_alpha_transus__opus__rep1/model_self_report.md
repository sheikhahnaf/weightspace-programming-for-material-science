# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> From the binary facts I noticed that 1/T for the alpha-gone temperature is close to linear in composition (T = 1155/(1+u), the dilute-solution form), and that the ternaries are near-additive in u apart from a few pair interactions. Units 1-5 build u as a linear index and turn it into a convex piecewise-linear T(u) that bottoms out at the 773 K floor. The small pair interactions (Al-Mo, Al-Nb, Al-Zr, Zr with beta stabilisers, low-level Sn-beta) are folded into u by linearising around the dataset's mean composition. The large ones are added in temperature space as separate ReLU units: Al-Sn (exact bilinear on the Sn grid), Al-V (a superlinear corner), Mo-Nb (exact bilinear on the Mo grid), and a Sn-with-high-beta term. By my hand checks the model should be within about 30 K on typical alloys. I expect larger errors, perhaps 50-100 K, for alloys that are very Al-rich with little beta stabiliser (underestimated) and for heavily loaded Mo-Nb alloys near the floor (overestimated). Confidence that the trends and scale are right: about 70%.
