From the listed solidus/liquidus pairs I computed the freezing range of each single-solute and two-solute alloy and saw that it is dominated by pairwise interactions (Al with Mo, Nb, Sn, V, Zr, and Mo with Nb), with small linear single-solute terms. I fitted a linear term per solute from the full-range binaries and a product coefficient per pair from the full-range ternaries (excess over the two singles), checking against the half-range points. The network encodes this as one linear unit plus two products, Al x (85 Mo + 38 Nb + 60 Sn + 132 V + 65 Zr) and Mo x (Nb + small V, Sn, Zr), each built from the identity ab = ((a+b)^2 - a^2 - b^2)/2 with piecewise-linear ReLU squares on knots spaced 0.5 in normalised units. Extrapolation of the pairwise quadratic model to alloys with four or more elements is an assumption that was not checked; no code or tools were used.
