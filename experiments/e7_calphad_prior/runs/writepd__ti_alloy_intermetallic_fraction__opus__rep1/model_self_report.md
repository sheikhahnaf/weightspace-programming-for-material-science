# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I built three ReLU units by hand from the sub-system facts. The first is a Ti3Al (alpha2) unit driven by an aluminium equivalent, Al + 1.5 Sn (about Rosenberg's coefficient in at.%), with a threshold near 12.5 at.% and small penalties for Mo, V, Nb and Zr. It is calibrated so that Ti-12Al-4Sn gives 0.57 while Ti-12Al, Ti-12Al-8Zr and Ti-12Al-9Mo give 0. The second is a B2 unit that needs high Al together with Nb, calibrated so that Ti-12Al-12Nb gives 0.73 while Ti-6Al-6Nb, Ti-12Al-12V and Ti-12Al-9Mo give 0; Sn lowers it because Sn stabilises alpha. The third unit caps B2 at about 0.9. I reproduce every binary and ternary fact exactly, but the multicomponent thresholds and the interplay between B2 and alpha2 are guesses: I am about 60% confident in the trends and 35% confident in the scale for quaternary and higher alloys.
