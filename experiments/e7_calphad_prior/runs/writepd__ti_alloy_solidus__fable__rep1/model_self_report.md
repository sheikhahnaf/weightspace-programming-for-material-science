# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I fitted the given sub-system solidus values by hand: unit 0 is an always-active linear unit (pure Ti 1941 K plus per-solute slopes in K per at.%), unit 1 gives the saturation of the Al effect above 6 at.%, and unit 15 the convexity in Sn. The remaining units encode pair interactions as min(a, k*b) = a - relu(a - k*b) terms plus relu(a + k*b - 12) hinges, with coefficients solved from the half-range and full-range binary-pair facts: strong negative Al-V, Al-Zr and Al-Sn terms, mild positive Al-Mo, Al-Nb, Mo-Nb and Mo-V terms, and small negative Sn-(Mo,Nb) and Mo-Zr terms. I checked by mental arithmetic that Ti-6Al and Ti-12Al-12V reproduce the listed values (1961 and about 1703 K); for alloys with four or more elements the pair terms are simply added, which is an assumption and may overstate the solidus drop in alloys rich in Al together with V, Zr and Sn. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies; I expect the trends and the scale to be right, with errors of some tens of K for typical alloys and more at the extreme corners.
