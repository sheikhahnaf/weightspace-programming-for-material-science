# Thermo-Calc licence: may the computed tables be published or redistributed?

Date: 2026-11-12. Question from the user: "find the thermo-calc licences question answer".

## Sources (fetched 2026-11-12)

1. Thermo-Calc Software AB, End User License Agreement (EULA), dated May 24, 2024, two pages: https://thermocalc.com/content/uploads/Documentation/Terms_Conditions/eula.pdf (the version for customers outside Europe). Grade: official page.
2. Thermo-Calc Software AB, General Terms and Conditions, dated April 27, 2021: https://www.thermocalc.com/media/6040/tcs-general-terms.pdf. Grade: official page.
3. Purchase terms page, which says that customers in Europe and elsewhere are under separate sets of agreements: https://thermocalc.com/terms-and-conditions-for-the-purchase/. Grade: official page.

Not read: the European EULA; the agreement Texas A&M actually signed (it may be an older or site-specific version and is the one that governs).

## What the EULA says (paraphrased; section numbers of the May 2024 EULA)

- 7(c), 7(d): the program and its results may be used only for the licensee's internal purposes; research for third parties counts as internal as long as supplying calculation results is not its sole or main purpose.
- 7(e): results may not be used to provide data or calculations as a service, or be incorporated into another product for redistribution, free or paid.
- 7(f)(i): whatever the licence type, written permission from Thermo-Calc Software is needed before making available to third parties, publishing, or storing in a public data repository "systematic mappings of results generated by the Program".
- 7(f)(ii): the same permission is needed to generate and disseminate look-up tables, surrogate models, neural networks or similar that are based on the program's results and could remove the need for the program or compete with it.
- 13: publications and reports that use results must acknowledge the program, to the extent section 7 allows such publications.
- General terms: third-party databases are additionally under their owners' licences. (TCHEA, TCNI, TCFE, TCTI and TCAL are Thermo-Calc's own databases.)

## Reading for this project (interpretation, not legal advice)

| Item | Reading |
|---|---|
| Results reported in the paper as figures, correlations and regret statistics | Ordinary publication of results; section 13 expects it, with acknowledgement. Low risk. |
| `data/calphad/` in a public repository (about 2,050 compositions x up to 5 targets, nine systems, plus sub-system facts) | Very likely a "systematic mapping of results" stored in a public data repository: needs written permission under 7(f)(i). |
| The facts blocks in the `writepd` prompts, and the one printed in the paper's supplement | Lists of computed binary and ternary values. Smaller, but the same clause could apply. |
| Networks written WITHOUT facts | Not based on the program's results (the model saw no Thermo-Calc output). 7(f)(ii) does not appear to apply. |
| Networks written WITH facts, and any GP fitted to the pools | Models based on the program's results. Disseminating them could fall under 7(f)(ii) if they are seen as removing the need for the program. We distribute the written networks (replies); we do not distribute fitted GPs. |
| The private GitHub repository | Not a public repository. Whether access by collaborators outside the licensed site counts as making results available to third parties depends on who has access. |

## What would settle it

Written permission from Thermo-Calc Software AB (info@thermocalc.com) for: (1) depositing the computed tables with the paper, and (2) releasing the networks written with sub-system facts. Until then a public release should use `scripts/make_release.py --without-thermocalc`, and should also consider leaving out the `writepd` replies and the facts example in the paper's supplement.
