16

Step 1

Prompt

Task in words, input names, an all-zero d → 16 → 1 network. No data.

Step 2

Written network

The model returns every weight as text; three replies per model and task.

Step 3

GP prior mean

Mean a·m(x) + b; the GP models what the network leaves over.

Step 4

Selection

At each step the reply most correlated with the labels so far; none below r = 0.3.

Step 5

BO loop

5 initial labels, 25 steps of expected improvement, 20 seeds.
