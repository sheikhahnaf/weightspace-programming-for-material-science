Prior mean

μ(x) = a · m(x) + b, where m(x) is the written network's output in physical units

GP on the residual

r = y − (a · m(x) + b) on the labelled alloys; exact GP fitted to r by marginal likelihood;
prediction = GP mean of r + a · m(x) + b; predictive standard deviation = the GP's own

Slope fitted

a and b by least squares on the labelled alloys.
An uninformative network drives a toward 0,
which gives back the constant-mean GP.
Unstable on about ten rows.

Slope fixed at one

a = 1; b = mean of y − m(x).
Trusts the scale of the written network.
Only the offset is taken from the labels.
