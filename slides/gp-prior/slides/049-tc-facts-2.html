Sub-system facts

Thermo-Calc results for sub-systems of at most three elements; no pool alloy, since every pool alloy has four or more.
High-entropy systems: every pure element, every binary at 25, 50 and 75 at.%, every equimolar ternary.
Balance systems: the pure base, each solute at half and at the whole of its range, each pair of solutes likewise.
31 to 105 facts per system: solidus, liquidus and the stable phases with their fractions at the fixed temperature.

Sub-system extrapolation (no LLM)

High-entropy systems, Redlich-Kister-Muggianu form:
rule of mixtures
+ x_i x_j [L0 + L1 (x_i − x_j) + L2 (x_i − x_j)²] per binary
+ x_i x_j x_k L_ijk per ternary.
Balance systems: base value + a·x + q·x² per solute
+ x_i x_j [b0 + b1 (x_i + x_j)] per pair of solutes.
Every coefficient is set exactly by the facts;
nothing is fitted to a multicomponent alloy.
