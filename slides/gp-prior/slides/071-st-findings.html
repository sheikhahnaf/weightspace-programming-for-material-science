Foundbulk modulus at 5 labels: ρ 0.15 for the embedding GP, 0.95 and 0.93 with an Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.2 network as its mean; the largest gain of the three cases
Foundthe gain is not the extra columns: the 15 descriptors added to the embedding as GP inputs give 0.30 to 0.52 at 5 to 20 labels, below the embedding alone from 10 labels on
Foundthe 16 written hidden units as GP inputs reach 0.94 with the whole pool, against 0.88 for the 50 embedding coordinates
Foundas a screening filter the Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 networks rank generator samples at ρ 0.88 to 0.92; the volume rule at 0.77 and 0.82
Limitfrom 20 labels the rule 1 / volume is as good or better (two-stage fit: 0.93 against 0.91 and 0.89; under the joint fit the rule is at 0.82); with the whole pool no mean differs from the plain GP (0.88 to 0.92)
Limitheat capacity: the Dulong-Petit rule ranks at 1.00 and the networks only match it
Limitthe targets come from a machine-learned potential, not from DFT or experiment
Limitranking only: no optimisation loop was run with a written mean on these tasks; the networks were written for the filter and reused here
