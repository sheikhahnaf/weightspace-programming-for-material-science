# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> I looked for w0 weights whose increase lowers the bulk modulus (negative dK) while having small energy effect (small dE mean and small dE sd), then raised eight of them (indices 3, 7, 14, 28, 51, 53, 85, 109) by 0.35 each. Their summed first-order effect is about -1.5 GPa on the mean bulk modulus, with a mean energy shift near zero and a per-crystal spread I estimated at roughly 5 meV per atom, so it is a cautious partial fix rather than a full correction of the +4.7 GPa bias. Confidence that the bulk modulus error shrinks without breaking the energy limit is about 55%; the estimate of the energy spread is rough and may be a bit optimistic.
