# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> The bias is an overestimate of K, so I looked for w0 weights whose increase lowers K while changing energy little. Weight 75 lowers K by about 1.78 GPa per unit with energy +0.0117 eV/atom per unit; weight 10 raises energy by a negative amount (-0.0312 per unit) with K +0.89, so raising both by 2.0 and 0.748 cancels the energy shift almost exactly (energy is linear, so the net change is about 0 meV/atom). The predicted K shift is about -2.9 GPa, short of the -4.7 GPa bias, and the linear estimate is shaky at this step size. Confidence that this reduces the error on new crystals: about 25%.
