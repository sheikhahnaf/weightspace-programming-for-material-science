Because energy is exactly linear in w0, I looked for a pair of weights whose energy effects cancel while their bulk-modulus effects add. Raising w0[37] by 0.5 lowers K by about 1.45 GPa but raises energy by 0.0276 eV per atom; raising w0[39] by 3.58 cancels that energy exactly (-0.0276 eV) and lowers K by about 3.7 GPa more, for an estimated total of roughly -5 GPa. The bulk-modulus numbers are only valid for small changes, so these large moves are an extrapolation. Confidence that this reduces the bulk-modulus error on new crystals without moving energies: about 25%.
