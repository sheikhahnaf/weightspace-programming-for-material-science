I aimed to remove most of the +2.3 GPa mean bias by shifting the mean bulk modulus down about 2.0 GPa. I used the near-dead features (small |w| and small energy sd: 64, 102, 59, 126, 43, 121, 34, 53) because they give the largest bulk-modulus change per unit of energy spread. Each step was sized as proportional to dK_mean / (dK_sd^2 + 0.1*dE_sd_meV^2), which trades the added scatter in K against the energy spread. b was lowered by 0.00423 to cancel the predicted +2.65 meV/atom mean energy shift. If the features are uncorrelated, the estimate is about 2.5 meV energy sd (mean absolute about 2 meV) and about 0.8 GPa of added K scatter. I am about 60% confident the MAE drops (modestly, to roughly 6.7-6.9 GPa) while staying inside the energy limit.
