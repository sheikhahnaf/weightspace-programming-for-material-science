Attempt 1 removed about 2.0 GPa of bias with a roughly proportional (-1.4%) correction but left the MAE flat, which suggests the error distributions are skewed (median below mean) and that the full shift overshot, especially in the 201-346 GPa range. I therefore aimed for a smaller mean shift of about -1.36 GPa, built mostly from near-zero weights whose bulk-modulus effect is close to proportional to K and carries little per-crystal scatter (w126, w43, w59, with a little w64). I set b to cancel the mean energy shift exactly; the sum of |change| x energy sd bounds the mean absolute energy change at about 4.3 meV, inside the limit. I am about 55% confident that this beats attempt 1's 6.99 GPa MAE, and I expect any gain to be small (a few hundredths of a GPa).
