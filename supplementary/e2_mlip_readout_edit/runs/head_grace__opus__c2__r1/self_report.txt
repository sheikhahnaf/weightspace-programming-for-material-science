The bias is small next to the MAE, and every weight that moves K also moves energy, so I ranked weights by |dK mean| / dE sd. I then changed 16 of them (w30, w37, w7, w6, w24, w50, w17, w45, w39, w46, w3, w38, w48, w19, w18, w23) in proportion to dK/sd^2, each in the direction that lowers K. I raised w25, a nearly constant feature, by 0.031 to cancel the resulting +3.1 meV/atom mean energy shift. If the features vary independently, the predicted effect is about -0.45 GPa on mean K with a mean absolute energy change of about 3.4 meV/atom. The energy figure depends on correlations between features that I cannot see, so this attempt mainly serves to calibrate the scale for later attempts. I am about 65% confident it stays within the 5 meV limit, and expect only a small MAE improvement (a few hundredths to about 0.1 GPa).
