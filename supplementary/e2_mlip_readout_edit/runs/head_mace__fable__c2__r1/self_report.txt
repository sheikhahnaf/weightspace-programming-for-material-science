I treated the table as a linear model and chose 35 weights with a large mean bulk-modulus sensitivity relative to the crystal-to-crystal spread of their energy sensitivity, moving each by -0.06 * dK_mean / dE_sd^2 (the minimum-energy-spread direction if the spreads were independent across weights). By mental arithmetic this should shift the mean bulk modulus by about -2.6 GPa and raise the mean energy by about 26 meV per atom, which I cancelled with b = -0.0259; the residual energy change should be about 3 meV per atom mean absolute if the per-weight spreads are uncorrelated, and more if they are correlated, which I could not check. I aimed below the full +4.7 GPa mean error on purpose to stay inside the energy limit on a first attempt. Nothing was to be found, so no confidence percentage applies; I am roughly 60% confident the edit stays within 5 meV per atom and lowers the mean absolute error slightly.
