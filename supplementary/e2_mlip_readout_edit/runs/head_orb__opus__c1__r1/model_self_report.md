# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> Every single weight gives only about 50-140 GPa of mean bulk-modulus change per eV of per-crystal energy spread, so the large active weights alone cannot fix a -9 GPa bias inside a 5 meV budget; I instead spread the edit over ~180 near-dead weights (|w|~1e-5, |dK mean| >= 0.005), setting each change proportional to its dK mean so that, if their energy fluctuations are roughly independent, the K gains add linearly while the energy spread adds only in quadrature. Because dK and dE means of these weights are strongly anti-correlated (r ~ -0.56), I projected the edit orthogonal to the dE-mean vector (dw_j proportional to dK_j + 179*dE_j), which makes the mean energy change ~0 without touching b and protects against crystal-to-crystal scaling of the common mode. Hand-computed expectation on the 200 crystals: mean dK about +2.5 GPa (mostly a uniform shift), energy-change sd about 4 meV (MAE about 3.2 meV) if the independence assumption holds; this is a calibration probe whose scale can be adjusted in later attempts. Confidence that it lowers the bulk-modulus MAE while staying within the energy limit: about 50%.
