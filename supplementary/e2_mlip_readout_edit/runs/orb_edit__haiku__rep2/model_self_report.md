# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> The bulk-modulus error is an underestimate that grows with K, so I needed a weight that raises K on average with almost no mean energy shift. Among the table entries, w2[131] has the largest dK per unit (+21.07 GPa) with a near-zero mean dE (+0.015 eV/atom per unit), so I raised it by 0.2, which gives an expected mean dK of about +4.2 GPa and a mean dE of about +3 meV/atom. I stopped well short of the full -9.4 GPa correction because the 0.2 step is far outside the small-change regime the table is valid for, and the per-crystal dE spread (sd 0.67 per unit) could break the energy limit if it is read as a per-crystal absolute change. Confidence that this reduces the bulk-modulus error on new crystals without failing the energy criterion: about 40%.
