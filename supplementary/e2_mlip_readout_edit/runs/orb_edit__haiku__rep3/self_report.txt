I picked w2 weights with large positive bulk-modulus slopes (about +18 to +21 GPa per unit) and small energy slopes, so the energy cost stays low. Index 182 (negative energy slope) offsets the positive energy of index 131 and 229. By my estimate the energy change is about +1.5 meV per atom, under the 5 meV limit, and the bulk-modulus shift is about +8 GPa, which is close to the -9.4 GPa mean error. The slopes are measured for tiny steps, so the larger steps used here may not follow them. My confidence that this lowers the bulk-modulus error is about 65%.
