# What the model said it did

Written by the model after its reply; not verified beyond the scored reply itself.

> The potential underestimates the bulk modulus, so I looked for weights with a large positive dK per unit and a near-zero mean dE (131, 229, 182, 70, 37). I raised each by 0.1, which by the linear table gives about +9.8 GPa in mean K, matching the mean error of -9.4 GPa. Their mean dE contributions nearly cancel (about +0.0004 eV per atom in total), so the mean energy shift should stay well under 5 meV. The per-crystal energy spread is larger (a few tenths of an eV in sd per unit weight), and the K change is only linear for small steps, so I am about 55% confident the edit reduces the error on new crystals without breaking the energy constraint.
