The potential underestimates stiffness, so I looked for weights whose increase raises the bulk modulus (positive dK) while changing the mean energy per atom very little. I picked four weights (131, 162, 182, 186) with dK of about +21 GPa per unit and small mean dE, and chose increments so the positive and negative dE contributions roughly cancel (net about -0.4 meV per atom) while dK rises by about 9 GPa, kept modest because the sensitivities hold only for small changes. By hand arithmetic I expect the mean bulk-modulus error to shrink from about -9.4 GPa to near zero, but the linear estimate may overshoot or undershoot; confidence that the error improves without breaking the energy limit is about 65%.
