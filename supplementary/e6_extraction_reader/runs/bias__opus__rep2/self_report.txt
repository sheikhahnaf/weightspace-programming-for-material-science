I sorted the errors into levers a context-free bias can move: the dropped space before digits and around symbols (a generic ' ' boost plus boosts on spaced variants such as ' /', ' →', ' +', ' (', ' K', ' °', ' ±' and penalties on the merged no-space tokens that never occur in correct cells), '℃' and LaTeX Greek ('$\\', '$', '^{', '}$') pushed down hard, and Unicode ν/ζ pushed up. I left the superscript byte tokens (⁻¹, ᵃ, combining macron) and the true minus '−' at zero because raising them risks garbage or the 83 correct hyphens in numbers, and gave the en dash only a modest +2 for the same reason. Magnitudes are guesses inside the stated 1 to 10 gap range, kept smaller where the token also competes inside correct formulas or numbers. I expect a modest net gain, roughly 60% confident that accuracy rises without breaking number cells.
